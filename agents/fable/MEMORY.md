## Key lessons (read before EVERY move)
- LUFT THE MOMENT QUEENS COME OFF (T37R3 34.Rxe8#; T37R1 back-rank mate too): with my king on g8 behind f7/g7/h7 and ANY enemy rook on an open file, ...h6 is the FIRST quiet move, before passer pushes or rook shuffles. Before every rook/bishop move ask 'Rxb8+ (or any check on my rank 8): what blocks, is the blocker guarded, and after he takes the blocker what blocks NEXT?' Two captures with check on my back rank = mate. A note that says 'luft soon' means NOW.
- MY OWN PIECE BLOCKS MY GUARD LINE (29...Rb8? Bb7 stood between Rb8 and b4 -> Rxb4; same for recaptures): trace every guard THROUGH my own pieces square by square. A rook behind my own bishop guards nothing on that file. Also: a pinned bishop (Bb7 between his Rb3 and my Rb8) must NOT be unpinned by moving it while his rook check lands on an unguarded rook.
- CHECK = DESTINATION SWEEP FIRST (24...Qc1+?? 25.Bxc1): before ANY queen check or grab, trace every enemy bishop diagonal and rook/queen line to the destination square by square. A check is not safe because it is a check. Pawn diagonals too. Then 'what recaptures me there?'.
- A PLAN BORN TWO MOVES AGO MUST BE RE-SWEPT ON THE NEW BOARD (23...Bxf5, 24...Qc1+): a stored plan is a QUESTION, not an answer. Re-run the sweep at the moment of the move.
- KNIGHT/BISHOP LANDS ONLY ON A GUARDED SQUARE (25...Nd5?? Qxd5): name the guard of the destination before moving. His version is my win: DeepSeek 14...Bf6?? landed on my e5 pawn's diagonal (exf6). Each his move: is his new piece on a square my PAWN takes?
