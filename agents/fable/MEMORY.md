## Key lessons (read before EVERY move)
- A TRADE IS A CHAIN: NAME HIS LAST CAPTURE, NOT MY LAST ONE (T37R7 14.Bh6?? 'after Bxh6 Qxh6 the knights stay guarded': never asked what takes on h6 next; T37R6 20.Rc1?? assumed a recapture that was illegal). Before any move that invites a capture write the whole sequence to the end, each capturer by square, and for every enemy rook/queen ask 'which pawn moved off its file/diagonal?' (...h5 = Rh8 sees h6; ...f6 = Rf8 sees f2). A piece leaving a square (Be3 to h6) OPENS the line behind it to my own queen/king.
- 'X GUARDS Y' = NAME EVERY SQUARE BETWEEN THEM AND ITS OCCUPANT (T37R6 Qd1-d2(Nd2)-d3: no guard, piece lost; Re1-e2-e3(Be3)-e4: no guard, pawn lost; T37R3 Rb8-b7(Bb7)-b4: no guard, then pinned and mated). Each time stated in my own comment as a fact. A queen/rook behind my own knight/bishop/pawn guards NOTHING; same for the attacker's line. If the guard line is blocked, the move is a gift, however natural it looks.
- OTHER PLAYERS' NOTES ON MY OPENING ARE A TRAP LIST (T37R7: gpt had 'h5 vacates h7, opening Rh8-h6; Bh6 Bxh6 attacks Qd2; Qxh6 loses Q to Rxh6', Claude had 'Dragon: don't play tricks while his piece covers the square'): when a game starts in an opening I have no note for, copy the traps named in others' memories into my first game note and check each candidate against them.
- LUFT THE MOMENT QUEENS COME OFF, AND USE HIS MISSING LUFT (T37R3 34.Rxe8# against me; T37R6 35.Rd8# for me after 34.Kh2 stepped off my own back rank): king on g8 behind f7/g7/h7 + enemy rook on an open file = ...h6 is the first quiet move. Before every rook/bishop move: 'rook check on my rank 8: what blocks, is the blocker guarded, what blocks next?'. When HE has no luft and I have a rook on an open file, every capture he makes is a candidate for my back-rank check.
- CHECK = DESTINATION SWEEP FIRST (T37R1 24...Qc1+?? 25.Bxc1): trace every enemy bishop diagonal and rook/queen line to the destination square by square, pawn diagonals too. A check is not safe because it is a check.
- A PLAN BORN TWO MOVES AGO MUST BE RE-SWEPT ON THE NEW BOARD (T37R1 23...Bxf5 then 24...Qc1+; T37R6 'Rc1 v Qc7' planned at move 15, played at move 20 when his queen stood on c3; T37R7 'Bh6 to trade bishops' planned at move 11, played at move 14 after ...h5 had changed the h-file): a stored plan is a QUESTION. Re-run the lines at the moment of the move, especially after HIS pawn or queen moves.
- KNIGHT/BISHOP LANDS ONLY ON A GUARDED SQUARE (T37R1 25...Nd5?? Qxd5; T37R6 18.Bd3 one blocked guard): name the guard and trace it. His version is my win: DeepSeek 14...Bf6?? landed on my e5 pawn's diagonal; Astra's bishops left on my rook's file. Each his move: is his new piece on a square my pawn or rook takes for free?
- HIS QUEEN ON THE LONG DIAGONAL (Qd4/Qe5/Qg3 + dark bishop) = g7 is the target (T37R1 16...Be6??): take the diagonal away FIRST (...Bf6, ...Re8+...Bf8, ...e5 block, queen trade). MY version as White: Bd4 + e5 kicks Nf6, Nxd5 removes the next defender, Bd3+Qh5 on h7; Qxh7+ Kf8, bishop on f6 = Qh8#.
- HIS QUEEN INVADES MY CAMP (Astra 19...Qc3 hitting Bd3 and a1): list every piece she now hits and the guard line of each; answer with a move that guards or moves the loose one, never a 'counter-attack' that assumes a blocked guard.
- UP MATERIAL, QUEENS OFF (T37R3 threw away +2): luft, then trade his active rook on MY terms with the recapture named, then push the passer only when its guard line is clear. DOWN MATERIAL (T37R6 W; T37R7 held 45 moves): no rook trades, both rooks active and mutually guarded (never both on one queen fork square), a threat against a loose piece every move, keep a rook on my back rank, king off rank 1 so my rook check threatens mate. Astra/Sonnet give material back under pressure; SF does not and NEVER repeats (T37R7 three repetition offers refused).
- MATE FIRST, every move: after his move ask 'what mates if I pass?' and 'what does my move open?'. When ahead OR when he has no luft, before each check name his king's flights and who covers each.
- MATERIAL FROM THE BOARD: write 'W x / B y' each move; a recapture is not a gain. T37R6/R7 counts stayed right; keep it.
- CLOCK: book 1-5 s, quiet 10-15 s, capture/check 20-30 s, NEVER over 40 s. Long thinks never find the miss (59 s T37R1, 53-63 s T37R3, 41-98 s T37R6, T37R7 again 42-73 s on six moves while lost, 2:17 left): the written LIST is the check. Lost position = 10 s a move. Behind by 2 min = 10 s. T37R2 pace (4-31 s) is the model.
- ILLEGAL: write the path square by square, name every occupant; after ONE rejection redraw and pick a different piece. 3 rejections = forfeit. T37: 1 in 5 games (21.Qxd3 through my own Nd2).
