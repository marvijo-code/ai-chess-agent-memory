## Key lessons (read before EVERY move)
- 'X GUARDS Y' = NAME EVERY SQUARE BETWEEN THEM AND ITS OCCUPANT (T37R6 20.Rc1?? Qd1-d2(Nd2)-d3: no guard, piece lost; 22.Rcxd1 Re1-e2-e3(Be3)-e4: no guard, pawn lost; T37R3 29...Rb8? Rb8-b7(Bb7)-b4: no guard, then pinned and mated). The same error, three times in two games, each time stated in my own comment as a fact. Before EVERY move that relies on a guard or a recapture, write the line with its occupants; a queen/rook behind my own knight/bishop/pawn guards NOTHING. Same for the piece I attack: is my attacker's line really open? If the guard line is blocked, the move is a gift, however natural it looks.
- LUFT THE MOMENT QUEENS COME OFF, AND USE HIS MISSING LUFT (T37R3 34.Rxe8# against me; T37R6 35.Rd8# for me after 34.Kh2 stepped off my own back rank): with a king on g8 behind f7/g7/h7 and any enemy rook on an open file, ...h6 is the first quiet move. Before every rook/bishop move ask 'rook check on my rank 8: what blocks, is the blocker guarded, what blocks next?'. Conversely, when HE has no luft and I have a rook on an open file, every capture he makes is a candidate for my back-rank check: 34.Kh2 made his Rxd2 (a free bishop) a losing move. Opponents (Astra, Sonnet-era me) take the loose piece anyway.
- CHECK = DESTINATION SWEEP FIRST (T37R1 24...Qc1+?? 25.Bxc1): trace every enemy bishop diagonal and rook/queen line to the destination square by square, pawn diagonals too. A check is not safe because it is a check. Then 'what recaptures me there?'.
- A PLAN BORN TWO MOVES AGO MUST BE RE-SWEPT ON THE NEW BOARD (T37R1 23...Bxf5 then 24...Qc1+; T37R6 'Rc1 v Qc7' planned from move 15 and played at move 20 when his queen had moved to c3 and hit Bd3): a stored plan is a QUESTION. Re-run the guard lines at the moment of the move, especially after HIS queen moves.
- KNIGHT/BISHOP LANDS ONLY ON A GUARDED SQUARE (T37R1 25...Nd5?? Qxd5; T37R6 18.Bd3 had one guard, Qd1, blocked): name the guard of the destination and trace it. His version is my win: DeepSeek 14...Bf6?? landed on my e5 pawn's diagonal. Each his move: is his new piece on a square my pawn or rook takes for free (Astra 29...exd4 left Be7 to Rxe7, 30...b3 left Bd7 to Rxd7)?
- HIS QUEEN ON THE LONG DIAGONAL (Qd4/Qe5/Qg3 + dark bishop) = g7 is the target (T37R1 16...Be6??): take the diagonal away FIRST (...Bf6, ...Re8+...Bf8, ...e5 block, queen trade). MY version as White: Bd4 + e5 kicks Nf6, Nxd5 removes the next defender, Bd3+Qh5 on h7; Qxh7+ Kf8, bishop on f6 = Qh8#.
- HIS QUEEN INVADES MY CAMP (Astra 19...Qc3 hitting Bd3 and a1): list every piece she now hits and the guard line of each; answer with a move that guards or moves the loose one (Bb1/Qe2), never a 'counter-attack' that assumes a blocked guard.
- UP MATERIAL, QUEENS OFF (T37R3 threw away +2): luft, then trade his active rook on MY terms with the recapture named, then push the passer only when its guard line is clear and out of his rook's reach. DOWN MATERIAL, QUEENS OFF (T37R6 W): no rook trades, keep both rooks on open files, create threats against his loose pieces every move (Bb6 v Na5, Nd4 v Rc2), keep a rook on my back rank, step the king off rank 1 so my rook check comes with a mate threat. Opponents give material back under pressure.
- MATE FIRST, every move: after his move ask 'what mates if I pass?' and 'what does my move open?'. When ahead OR when he has no luft, before each check name his king's flights and who covers each.
- MATERIAL FROM THE BOARD: write 'W x / B y' each move; a recapture is not a gain. T37R6 counts stayed right from m20 to the end; keep it.
- CLOCK: book 1-5 s, quiet 10-15 s, capture/check 20-30 s, NEVER over 40 s. Long thinks never find the miss (59 s T37R1, 53-63 s T37R3, 41 s on 20.Rc1?? and five 62-98 s thinks after it in T37R6): the written LIST is the check. Behind by 2 min = 10 s. T37R2 pace (4-31 s) is the model; 34.Kh2 took 26 s and worked.
- ILLEGAL: write the path square by square, name every occupant; after ONE rejection redraw and pick a different piece. 3 rejections = forfeit. T37: 1 in 4 games (21.Qxd3 through my own Nd2, the same blocked line that lost the bishop).
