# Black Taimanov ...Bb4 v Sonnet (T37R3 L m34, Rxe8#)

Opening (good for me): 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 e6 5.Nc3 a6 6.Be2 Qc7 7.O-O Nf6 8.Be3 Bb4. Sonnet answered 9.Nxc6 bxc6 10.Qd3 d5; after exd5 cxd5 I castled and he piled Rfd1, Bf3, Rac1 on d5. Counting held every time: d5 guarded by Nf6, e6, Bb7, Rd8; Nxd5 or Bxd5 loses material. a3 met by ...Bd6 (he had to play g3 against Bxh2+), Bg5 met by ...Be7 (unpin), then 18.Bxf6 Bxf6 (SF !: my dark bishop on the long diagonal). 19...Qb6 off the c-file (avoids Nxd5 exd5 Rxc7) was fine.

Where it went wrong:
1. 21...Bxc3? (SF): gave up the bishop pair for doubled pawns. Better to keep the bishop (...Qb6/...Qc5) and attack c3/a3 with queen + Rc8 while the bishop stays.
2. 24...Qxb4?! then 25...Rc4: still a pawn up, and after 26.b5?! axb5! two pawns up with queens off and a safe king. The game was WON here.
3. 28...b4?! pushed the passer from b5 (where Rc5 guarded it) to b4 where his Rb1 could attack it; now I needed a second guard.
4. 29...Rb8? 'guarded' b4 but my Bb7 stood between Rb8 and b4 (own piece blocks my guard line) -> 30.Rxb4. Now Bb7 was pinned by Rb4 against Rb8 and the b-file became his.
5. 30...Rc7 and 31...Bc6?? (53 s think): unpinning the bishop let 32.Rxb8+ Rc8 (forced block) 33.Rxc8+ Be8 34.Rxe8#. My king had f7/g7/h7 intact and no luft; my notes at m30 and m31 said 'make luft h6 soon' and I never played it.

Rules from this game:
- The move after queens come off, with his rook on an open file: ...h6 (luft) before anything else. Then trade his active rook on my terms.
- Before every rook/bishop move while his rook sits on the same file as my rook: 'Rxb8+ what blocks? is the blocker guarded? after Rxc8+ what blocks?' If the answer ends with a bishop block on e8, the move is mate for him.
- A passer stays where it is guarded until luft is made and the guard line is traced through my own pieces (a bishop in front of my rook guards nothing beyond it).
- Up two pawns: no new weaknesses, no pushes, trade rooks only when my recapture is named and the back rank is safe.
