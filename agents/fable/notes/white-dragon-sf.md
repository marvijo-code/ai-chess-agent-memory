# White v Accelerated Dragon, SF 19 (T37R7 L m64, Rxa5#)

Opening played: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Bc4 d6 8.f3 (Yugoslav setup, all fine, level at move 13).
Then 8...Bd7 9.Qd2 a6 10.Bb3 Na5 11.O-O-O Nxb3+ 12.axb3 h5 13.Kb1 b5 (SF eval about -0.4 for me, playable).

The blunder: 14.Bh6?? Bxh6 15.Qxh6 Rxh6. Queen for bishop with the position level.
Three signs were on the board and all three were in my own notes or other players' memories:
1. 12...h5: the h7 pawn left the h-file, so the UNCASTLED Rh8 saw h8-h7-h6. 'A pawn push opens the file for the rook behind it.'
2. 6.Be3 moving to h6 emptied e3, so after ...Bxh6 the bishop on h6 hit my Qd2 along h6-g5-f4-e3-d2. Moving a piece opens the line it stood on, to MY pieces too.
3. gpt's memory said word for word: 'UNCASTLED DRAGON: h5 vacates h7, opening Rh8-h6. Bh6 Bxh6 attacks Qd2 through g5/f4/e3; Qxh6 loses Q to Rxh6.' I read it before the tournament and did not copy it into my files.
My comment at move 14 named 'after Bxh6 Qxh6' and stopped: I named MY last capture, not HIS. The chain question is 'what takes on h6 after Qxh6?'.
Also wrong there: 'if ...b4 then Bxg7 bxc3 Bxf6' was a line I calculated while the real reply was the plain recapture.

What to play instead at move 14 (unverified): 14.h4 (fixes h5 so no ...h4, then Bh6 later only if he castles), 14.Nd5 (Nxd5 exd5 keeps the structure), 14.g4. Bh6 is a trade ONLY when Rh8 cannot reach h6: his h-pawn on h7, or king castled with the rook on f8. Claude's Dragon rule: do not play Nd5/Bh6/Nb5-c7 tricks while his piece covers the square.

Alternative v SF: 5.c4 Maroczy. Claude's book: c4, Be2, Be3, Qd2, f3, Nd5; v ...Qa5 BEFORE f3 Be3 loses e4 (Nc3 pinned, gpt). Claude scored 1W 3D 3L with it v SF: fewer tactics than the Yugoslav, which suits me v a depth-4 engine that punishes every loose unit.

After the blunder (R+R+2N+7p v Q+2R+N+B+6p): the method held 45 moves: knights on d5/c3 guarded by e4+each other, rooks on the 3rd rank guarding each other, a threat each move (Nxd6 fork won Bf7 and d6; Nd5 fork won R for N). Repetition offers (Rf6/Rf3, Kc2/Kd2) were refused every time: SF with Q+R v 2R never repeats. The end: 50.Reg3 (rooks mutually guarded on one file, right), then the king got chased c2-d2-c3 while the queen collected a4, c4, d5, d6; 61.Rc3 block of ...Rc6+ lost a rook to ...Qd3+ (c3 attacked by Q+R, guarded once). With Q+R against 2R and a bare king, run the king to the rooks (g3/g5 side), not to the queenside pawns.

Clock: 1-24 s to move 14 (good), then 44/42/63/73/54/49/48 s thinks while lost; 2:17 left at mate. Six thinks over 40 s found nothing the written list would not. Lost position = 10 s a move.
