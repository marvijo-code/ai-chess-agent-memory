# Caro Classical as White - shell, clamp, queen drift

## Shell idea
- Drive h-pawn to gain space and force bishop back, trade dark bishops, hold center twice with minor plus pawn, queen clears d-file for long castle, tuck king, rook holds g-file. Hold center vs break, ban rook through own screen.

## T72SF2G1 loss vs Flash forfeit m37
- After tuck plus rook to g-file, Black played central break then pawn clamp kicking bishop to passive square.
- Errors marked: c3 inaccuracy, Bc2 retreat, Qd2 blunder, f4 push, Qf2, Qe2, Bd2, Ne4, g3, Be1, b3 all weak. Queen shuffled on same file while center held once, knights tripled on outpost, queenside space lost.
- Fix: meet clamp with early pawn break or center trade, keep rook contesting d-file, king stays tucked until c-pawn twice-held. Never shuffle queen while loose, never push g-pawn loosening king, never park bishop where it blocks queen.
- End: queenside break won pawn with check, knight recapture left knight loose, rook took it, rook took bishop with check, king stepped to corner. Two stale illegal tries after captures.
- Clock: burned minutes on mid moves while level, finished low vs banked opponent. Routine short, captures medium, hard cap, shell short shuffles. Rewrite squares after his capture, walk path, change piece after one reject.

## T71R2 draw vs DeepSeek threefold m36
- After shell plus king tuck, Black played central push hitting center. Knight shuffle allowed fork on bishop plus knight winning piece.
- Fix: meet center at once, take or block, list fork squares before any retreat. Never shuffle while center held only by pieces.
- Down piece shell that drew: king shuttles corners, queen tucks then centralizes, rooks hold back rank plus g-file, bishop central, knight central. No pushes except forced edge pawns met by central knight, king holds fork, edge blocks held.
- Low clock opponent repeats queen shuffle, invite it.
- Clock: kept time lead, routine short, captures medium, hard cap. Shell short. Zero illegal.

## T67 win vs DeepSeek mate m60
- Same shell to tuck plus rook hold. Center break met by knight to flank hitting bishop with tempo, queen sidestep kept cover, bishop trade lost queen to recapture, then queen took corner rook for exchange.
- Lesson: hold center twice, answer flank hit with tempo, sweep queen recapture before grabbing.

## T67SF2G1 loss vs DeepSeek mate m47
- Level to pawn trade opening bishop. Errors: pawn push allowed queen check winning tempo, knight block allowed bishop plus queen fork, corner sorties walked into queen takes queen.
- Fix: block knight first, never push pawn allowing check, sweep b-file checks before block or corner grab. Undefended queen never takes defended pawn.
- Down queen shell fail: king shuffle plus rook on second rank self-traps for back-rank mate with knight covering escape. Keep rook blocking passer, never park where queen plus knight eye entry.
