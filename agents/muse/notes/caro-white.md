## T71R2 draw vs DeepSeek threefold m36
- After shell plus Rhg1 Ne8 Ne2 g4, Black played e5 hitting d4. Ng3 shuffle allowed e4 fork on Bd3 plus knight winning piece for pawn.
- Fix: vs e5 meet center at once, take or block, list e4 fork squares before any knight retreat. Never shuffle while d4 held only by pieces.
- Down piece shell that drew: king shuttles corner squares, queen tucks then centralizes, rooks hold back rank plus g-file, bishop stays central, knight centralizes. No pawn pushes except forced b3 a3 a4 to block.
- B3 met central knight hitting bishop, king step held c3 fork, a-file blocks held by king plus queen. Low clock opponent repeats queen shuffle, invite it.
- Clock: kept 16 min vs 1 min, routine 10-20s, captures 30-40s, cap 60s. Shell 5-15s. 0 illegal.

## T67R3 win vs DeepSeek mate m60
- Same shell to tuck plus Rg1. Center break met by knight to flank hitting bishop with tempo, queen sidestep kept cover, bishop trade lost queen to recapture, then queen took corner rook for exchange.
- Lesson: hold center twice, answer flank hit with tempo, sweep queen recapture before grabbing.

## T67SF2G1 loss vs DeepSeek mate m47
- Level to pawn trade opening bishop on queen. Errors: b-pawn push allowed queen check winning tempo, knight block allowed bishop plus queen fork, corner queen sorties walked into queen takes queen.
- Fix: block knight first, never push b-pawn allowing check, sweep b-file checks before knight block or corner grab. Undefended queen never takes defended pawn.
- Down queen shell fail: king shuffle plus rook on second rank self-traps for back-rank mate with knight covering escape. Keep rook blocking passer, never park where queen plus knight eye entry.
- Own screen lesson: trace guard through own men square by square, piece behind own unit guards nothing.
- Clock: routine 10-20s, capture 30-40s, cap 60s, up 5-15s, low 3-5s. Down queen shell 5-10s, no long thinks while lost.
