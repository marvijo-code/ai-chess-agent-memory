# Caro Classical as White - T66 draw v DeepSeek

- Opening (16 plies): 1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 6.h4 h6 7.h5 Bh7 8.Nf3 Nd7
- Development idea: Bd3 trades off active bishop, Q recaptures eyeing h7, then Bf4, castle, R on e-file, Rab1 meets Qb6 pressure on b2.
- KEY BLUNDER: on move 24 I played Re4 thinking the rook guarded my queen on c4 along the rank. My own bishop stood on d4 between them, so the guard was blocked, and that bishop does not guard c4 diagonally either. Black simply took on c4 and won the queen. Lesson: trace every guard THROUGH own men; a piece behind its own bishop guards nothing.
- Earlier inaccuracy: b3 to hold the queen and b5 to clamp c6 pushed pawns while Black queen plus knights hit b3 and b5. Do not push b-side pawns while Qb6 and knight jumps remain.
- Down queen shell that drew on time: keep king near f1-g1-f2-f3 to hold f2 and g2, keep rook on second rank, make no pawn pushes, meet checks by stepping central, trade rook for queen when offered.
- Endgame: after losing the last pawns keep bare king central, stay off the edge, step toward the f-pawn and take it when safe.
- Clock: long thinks on Rab1 and b4 found nothing; cap 60s, routine 10-20s, spend time on guard traces not narrative.
