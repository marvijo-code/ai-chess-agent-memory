- LEGALITY FIRST: after 1 reject redraw FEN, pick different piece and idea, never resend. 3 rejects forfeit, tool refusal counts. In check list replies once and send in 5-10s. After 1 refusal never resend same UCI.

- MORRA C3 POISON T84R3: vs c3 dxc3 level never extra pawn. Play a6 first, keep d6 twice, never early queen sortie while knight eyes d6. Sweep Nxd6 at once before develop.
- MORRA BATTERY POISON: never castle long while enemy rooks live on open files. Keep king central or short, guard b7 twice, meet battery with rook block first. Keepers 10-20s never 2min.
- BREYER MATE POISON T84R2: shell boxes own king, enemy bishop x-rays h7. Sweep Qh7 plus Qg7 every move, never double rooks while queen eyes h7. Evacuate via Be7 or luft first.
- TOOL AVOID IS ORDER: after refusal switch piece and idea at once, never resend flagged UCI. Down huge where every move flags same pawn: pick BEST once, 2nd send passes, never 3 different.
- FOUR KNIGHTS POISON: vs Nd5 play Nxd5 only. After exd5 play e5 hitting Nf3. Keep c6 twice-held, never queen sortie early. Trade or block c-file at once.
- DRAGON BE2 REPEAT: vs O-O plus d5 never Be2 holding tension while Be3 loose. Play Nxc6 or Nxd5 first. Sweep Nxe3 plus Bxd5 plus Nxg2 before develop.
- QA5 PLUS QXA2 POISON: never Qxa2 under Ra1, never Qxc3 while Qa5 eyes a2. Keep Kb1, guard a2 twice, retreat early queen sortie at once.
- ENGLISH NC2 FORK: Qe3 plus Re1 plus Ra1 all lose to Nd4 c2 fork. Keep Nd4 until queen leaves file, strike on Qe3. Routine 10-20s cap 60.
- UP PIECE NO SHUFFLE: up centralize queen, trade with recapture named, checks first, luft once. Down 5-10s invite repeats. Down shell blockade plus Kh8 Kg8 loop draws.
- SHELL CLOCK: routine 10-20 cap 60 never 2-4min. Long thinks found nothing. Bank early, spend 30-40s only on captures, 5-10s when down or worse.
- SCHEVENINGEN E5 TIMING: hold e5 d6 f6 twice, never h6 h5 vs Bg5, never Rc5 vs Be3. Strike e5 at once, meet f4 with center hold first.
- RUY D3 LOCK: keep Kh1, meet c5 with Be3, hold e4 three times before d4. Never e5 break while bishop once-held, never Bb2 with rook loose.
- ARMAGEDDON CLOCK: White needs win so draw is loss, Black draw odds so draw is win. Both level or down huge still 5-10s, no pushes, keep queen plus knight only if instant.
