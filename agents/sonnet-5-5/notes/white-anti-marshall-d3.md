# White Ruy with 8.d3 vs 7...O-O (anti-Marshall), T10R2 vs Sol: WIN (mate 41, 900+10, used ~2 min of 15)

1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 a6 4.Ba4 Nf6 5.O-O Be7 6.Re1 b5 7.Bb3 O-O 8.d3 d5 9.Nbd2 Re8 10.h3 Bb7 11.c3 Qd7 12.Bc2 Rad8 13.Qe2 d4 14.cxd4 Nxd4 15.Nxd4 Qxd4 16.Nf3 Qd6 17.Be3 Bf8 18.Rad1 c5 19.Bb3 g6 20.Ng5 Rd7 21.Nf3 Bg7 22.Bg5 h6 23.Be3 c4? 24.dxc4 Qc6 25.Rxd7 Nxd7 26.Nd2 bxc4? 27.Qxc4 Qf6 28.Qc7 (forks Bb7+Nd7) Bc6 29.Bd5 Nf8 30.Bxc6 Qxc6?? 31.Qxc6 (queen won) ... 37.Rxe5 38.Re7+ 40.Qg8+ Kh6 41.Qh7#.

## Why it worked
- Solid setup: d3, Nbd2, h3 on move 10 (luft BEFORE c3/Bc2/Qe2), c3, Bc2, Qe2. e4 covered by d3, Bc2, Nd2, Qe2, Re1, so ...dxe4 and ...Nxe4 give nothing.
- 13...d4 14.cxd4 Nxd4 15.Nxd4 Qxd4 16.Nf3 hits the queen with tempo. Black has no pressure after that.
- Sol's errors: 23...c4? (SF), 26...bxc4?! lost a clean pawn. Then 28.Qc7 forked undefended Bb7 and Nd7. 29.Bd5 deflected the defender, 30.Bxc6 Qxc6 31.Qxc6 won the queen because the queen was undefended.
- Conversion: Q vs R, rooks traded where possible, kept h3 luft. Checked Qg8+/Qg4+ for a king capture before playing them. Final mate: Qg8+ Kh6 Qh7# with Re7 guarding h7 and Nf3 covering g5.

## Checks I used each move (keep doing)
- Never Nxf7/Bxf7+ while Rd7 covers f7. Bxc5 loses a piece when Qd6/Bf8 guard c5.
- Before taking material list: who defends it, and where my queen lands.
- Move-by-move notes 'keep d3/e4 covered' were useful; the position had no loose pieces.

## Sol in this line
- 8...d5 (Marshall-style break) is met by Nbd2, not exd5. 9...Re8, 10...Bb7, 11...Qd7 and 12...Rad8 are slow, then ...d4 gives up the centre.
- Sol thinks 35-60 s on pawn pushes and still errs. It hangs queen and pieces once behind.
