## Scan (EVERY move, 5 s, FINAL position)
1. Legality: my turn; my pieces; geometry; PATH clear of EVERYTHING incl. my own pieces (g70 Be3 blocked by own Nd2, Bg5 by own Ne3 - 2 tries burned); never echo his move or repeat a piece's current square; two pieces to one square -> full name (Rfd8); one rejected attempt -> different move; 3 invalid = forfeit. 2. HIS last move FIRST: every enemy attacker of my destination - rook file/rank, BISHOP DIAGONALS, knights, PAWNS (g68 Ra6?? Bd3 covers a6; g73 23.Be5?? dxe5, d6-pawn - I noted 'eyed by Be7' and missed the pawn). Attacked+undefended -> reject. Pawn capture squares apply to EVERY piece move, not only captures.
3. His KING hits its 8 neighbours (g55 Rd2?? Kxd2); his rook on my 2nd rank: moving a blocker drops the piece behind. Save my hit/loose piece NOW.
4. Pawns: never onto a pawn-attacked square even if defended; is this pawn my piece's sole guard? Recapture MY push (g63 b5?? axb5)? Push opens a file - who enters first (g61 b4? axb3 Rxa1; Nxb3! holds)?
5. QUEEN: enemy PAWNS'/KNIGHTS' capture squares FIRST, then N/B/R/Q lines; no queen on a line facing his rook/queen (g61 Qa3?? Rxa3; g63 Qa5?? Rxa5). Qd4 vs his e5-pawn = Q for P (g67, g70). Sac: every recapturer + net (g65 Bxh6 = B+Q for 2P). Recount an advanced pawn's guards before a trade beside it (g67 d5).
7. Loose minors/rooks: before a jump, retreat or rook swing list every enemy attacker of the destination + chain; destination attacked by NOTHING (g58 Nd4??; g63 Nb4?? cxb4; g68 Ra6??; g70 Bd3?? Nxd3; g73 Bd3?? Rxd3).

9. Down material: defend/trade, 5-15 s, no undefended pieces, no 'active' queen onto a pawn-attacked square (g70); pawn down -> keep queens (g68 Be6? Qxd8); repetition/stalemate = half point; when winning make progress.
10. Time: routine <=15 s, book <=10 s; <3 min -> <=5 s; <1 min -> 1-2 s; when lost 1-5 s. 30-90 s thinks never prevented a blunder or an illegal try (g50, g59, g61-g73).

## Patterns
- Queen: onto a pawn-attacked/covered square, in front of his rook on an open line/file, or with no recapturer (g48-g51, g56-g61, g63, g67, g70, g73).
- 'Active/centralizing' piece onto a pawn's capture square (g73 Be5?? dxe5) - activity is not safety.
- Covered-unit grabs; captures whose follow-up is blocked; sacs with 2+ recapturers (g65); f2-battery (g61); h7 nets (g66, g69).
- Rook/knight/bishop onto a pawn-attacked or bishop/knight-covered square (g68 Ra6, g70 Bd3, g73 Bd3).
- Sending a self-banned move (g67, g70); 2-3 illegal tries in complex middlegames (g68, g70) - trace path and destination twice.
- When winning: no repetition drift; keep every unit defended (Sonnet takes all).
