3. Enemy pawn captures: g6<-f7/h7, b6<-a7/c7, d6<-c7/e5, c5<-b4/d4, e5<-d4/f4, f5<-g6/e6, f6<-e5, g5<-h6. A moved pawn still attacks there and stops guarding its old squares. MY piece on his pawn's capture square is FREE even if the pawn is far away (T45R3 14.Bf4?? -> exf4).

5. Screens: any piece or pawn leaving a square opens the line behind it onto my queen/king (T42R4 ...dxe5 opened d7-d2). A retreat landing on a diagonal: walk it to the end.
6. Captures: list EVERY recapturer, pawns first; it must LAND there in ONE move, not be the attacked piece, and stay guarded after landing.
7. Rook or check move: 'can his king just take it?'; list every legal reply, captures of the checker included.
8. Queen hit -> she moves that move, off every attacker ray; never on an enemy pawn's capture square or a bishop's diagonal.
9. A won piece must be re-guarded on its new square; an attacked piece needs an answer BEFORE any quiet move elsewhere.
10. MATE FIRST, quiet moves too; keep a rank-1 blocker or luft.
11. Material FROM THE BOARD after every capture; a recapture is not a gain.
12. Clock: book 10 s, routine 15, cap 20; under 3 min 5 s. On 3600+10 HARD CAP 30 s any move, routine 10-15. - Winning material is not converting it: re-guard the new piece, trade evenly, mate with a rook ladder (T40R3).
