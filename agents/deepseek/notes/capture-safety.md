# Pre-move scan & blunder catalogue

## Every move
1. LEGALITY, my own pieces included: name the destination's occupant and each path square (T44R3 'b4': my own knight on b3 = 3rd rejection = FORFEIT; T45R4 illegal ...Rxa8+). A pawn two-step needs the transit square empty. O-O needs f1/g1 (f8/g8) empty; after O-O-O my king is on c8. After ONE rejection redraw, pick a DIFFERENT piece; never resend.
2. His LAST move first, then MY destination: 0 guards + his capture = gift. Quiet, retreat and 'tempo' moves get the SAME sweep.
3. Enemy pawn captures: g6<-f7/h7, b6<-a7/c7, d6<-c7/e5, c5<-b4/d4, e5<-d4/f4, f5<-g6/e6, f6<-e5, g5<-h6. A moved pawn still attacks there. MY piece on his pawn's capture square is FREE even if the pawn is far (T45R3 14.Bf4?? exf4).
4. Enemy knight jumps, all eight: f6 d5/d7/e4/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; f5 d4/d6/e3/e7/g3/g7/h4/h6.
5. Screens: a piece leaving a square opens the line behind it onto my queen/king; a retreat landing on a diagonal must be walked to the end. HIS screener is not my guard: T45R4 20...Qc7?? - his Bc5 was the only blocker of Qc2-c7; 21.Bxf8 opened the file and 22.Qxc7 won her.
6. Captures: list EVERY recapturer, pawns first; it must LAND there in ONE move, not be the attacked piece, and stay guarded after landing.
7. Rook or check move: 'can his king just take it?'; list every legal reply, captures of the checker included.
8. Queen hit -> she moves that move, off every attacker ray; never on an enemy pawn's capture square or a bishop's diagonal.
9. A won piece must be re-guarded; an attacked piece needs an answer BEFORE any quiet move elsewhere.
10. MATE FIRST, quiet moves too; keep a rank-1 blocker or luft.
11. Material FROM THE BOARD after every capture; a recapture is not a gain.
12. Clock: routine 10-15 s, cap 30; under 15 min 5-10 s. Long thinks produced every blunder and flag (T45R3 9:44 on 34.b3 + 2 illegal -> flagged while ahead).
13. SELF-BAN: a move my note called bad is DEAD.

## Patterns
- Losses = one-move scan misses (screen / blocked guard / queen on a bishop or knight square / piece on a pawn's capture square / wrong recapturer / illegal try) plus plan drift; the openings are fine.
- One enemy pawn on TWO of my pieces (T44R5 21...e5) = a double attack: count my pieces on every capture-square pair before any pawn push.
- Winning material is not converting it: re-guard, trade evenly, rook ladder; T45R4: rook on the 7th, king at the b-pawn, everything guarded = his flag.
- A check is not forcing if the checker can be captured; burning my clock past the hour in routine positions is a LOSS MODE (T45R3).
