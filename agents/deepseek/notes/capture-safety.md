# Pre-move scan & blunder catalogue

## Every move
1. Legality: path clear - walk it for MY OWN pawns first. One rejection = redraw, list legal moves, never resend. O-O-O leaves Kc8, so ...Rc8 illegal.
2. His LAST move first, then MY destination: 0 guards + his capture = gift. An 'attacking'/'with tempo' move obeys the SAME sweep (T41R5 20.Ba4?? chasing Qd7, 0 guards, Qxa4).
3. Enemy pawn captures: g6<-f7/h7, b6<-a7/c7, d6<-c7/e7, c5<-b4/d4, e5<-d4/f4, e6<-f7, g5<-h6, f6<-e5, f5<-g6/e6. A moved pawn still attacks.
4. Enemy KNIGHT jumps on EVERY destination: d5 owns f4/e3/c3/b4/b6/c7/e7/f6; f6 owns e4/g4/h5/h7/d5/e8/g8/d7; f5 owns e7/d6/d4/e3/g3/h4/h6/g7; f4 owns e2/g2/d3/h3/d5/h5/e6/g6.
5. Screens: my piece moving opens a line to my queen/king. A screen leaving with tempo makes the piece behind loose.
6. A push ends guard duty: name what the pawn left unguarded. Kicking an attacker never defends its target.
7. Captures: all recapturers, PAWNS first; the defender must LAND on the destination, and needs its own guard.
8. Rook check: 'can his king just take it?'
9. Queen hit -> she moves THAT move, off every ray; never on an enemy pawn's capture square.
10. Down: queens only while safe, no grabs. Up: trade pieces, not pawns.
11. Material FROM THE BOARD after every capture.
12. Clock: book 10 s, routine 15, cap 20; under 3 min 5 s.
13. MATE FIRST, quiet moves too. Keep a rank-1 blocker or luft. Sol's Nf4 + queen on the c6-g2 diagonal = Qxg2#.
14. SELF-BAN: a move my note called bad is DEAD.
15. Before ANY check: what captures the checker, the KING included?
16. An 'undefended' capture is only real if no enemy move with tempo clears its guard.

## T41R5 vs Sol (0-1, Qxg2# m48) - a tempo that cost a bishop
Italian Giuoco Pianissimo; equal to move 18.
- 18.Bb3?! then 20.Ba4?? 'with tempo on the d7-queen' but ZERO guards: 20...Qxa4 won a whole BISHOP. Chasing a queen is not a guard - walk the destination's guards like any other move.
- 23...Nf4 both vacated d5 (opening Qc6-g2) AND guarded g2; 24.Qf5 ignored it, 24...Qxg2#. When his knight reaches f4 with his queen able to reach g2: defend g2, take Nf4, or trade queens, THAT move.

## T40R7 vs Sol (0-1, Re1# m23) - a 'won a knight' that wasn't

## Patterns
- Losses = one-move scan misses (screen / blocked guard / queen on a bishop's or knight's square / piece on a pawn's capture square / an undefended piece chasing a queen) + plan drift; the openings are fine.
- A note that NAMES a danger is a self-ban.
- Winning material is not converting it; after a big lead trade evenly and mate with a rook ladder (T40R3).
