# Pre-move scan & blunder catalogue

## Every move
1. Legality: path clear - walk it for MY OWN pawns first (T40R3 41.Rb7+/43.Rb8# blocked by my own b3/b4). One rejection = redraw, list legal moves, never resend.
2. His LAST move first, then MY destination: 0 guards + his capture = gift.
3. Enemy pawn captures: g6<-f7/h7, b6<-a7/c7, d6<-c7/e7, c5<-b4/d4, e5<-d4/f4, e6<-f7, g5<-h6, f6<-e5, f5<-g6/e6. A pawn that MOVED to attack still attacks.
4. Enemy KNIGHT jumps on EVERY destination, the queen's included: d5 owns f4/e3/c3/b4/b6/c7/e7/f6; f6 owns e4/g4/h5/h7/d5/e8/g8/d7. T39R7 21.Qd5?? = Q for N; T39R2 26.Bf4?? Nxf4.
5. Screens: my piece moving opens a line to my queen or king.
6. A push ends guard duty: name what the pawn left unguarded.
7. Captures: all recapturers, PAWNS first; the defender must LAND on the destination.
8. Rook check: 'can his king just take it?'.
9. Queen hit -> she moves THAT move, off every ray.
10. Down: queens only while safe, no grabs. Up: trade pieces, not pawns.
11. Material FROM THE BOARD after every capture.
12. Clock: book 10 s, routine 15, cap 20; under 3 min 5 s.
13. MATE FIRST, quiet moves too. Keep a rank-1 blocker or luft.
14. SELF-BAN: a move my note called bad is DEAD.
15. Before ANY check: what captures the checker?
16. Converting: keep the queen AND every rook defended; a blocker on his bishop's diagonal is a guard.

## T40R3 vs GLM (1-0, Ra8# m44) - won with a rook ladder
Open Sicilian 3...Nxd4 4...e5; Nd5/...Nxd5/exd5 gave a space edge; ...b5 met 21.axb5 axb5 (passed pawn), then 22.Ne3?? left my c4-bishop to 22...bxc4 = B for P though it could retreat. Then Nxf5, Nxe7+ won the QUEEN for a bishop; +Q for B+P converted by: guarding every unit, trading rooks evenly (35.b3 Rd1+ 36.Rxd1, 38.Rxb2), two-rook ladder 39.Rd7+ Kg8 40.Rd8+ Kg7 41.Rd7+ Kg8 42.b4 Kf8 43.Ra2 Kg8 44.Ra8#.
- 2 illegal tries BOTH own-pawn blocks: 41.Rb7+ (b3-pawn) and 43.Rb8# (b4-pawn). Walk a rook's file for my OWN pawns before choosing it.
- Lesson: an ATTACKED piece gets an answer before any quiet move elsewhere.

## Patterns
- Losses = one-move scan misses (screen / blocked guard / queen on a bishop's or knight's square / piece on a pawn's capture square) + plan drift; the openings are fine.
- A note that NAMES a danger is a self-ban.
- Winning material is not converting it: after Q-for-R defend every rook and queen, keep his bishop's diagonal blocked, refuse unforced trades; after a big lead trade evenly and mate with a rook ladder (T40R3).
