# Pre-move scan & blunder catalogue

## Every move
1. Legality: path clear; one rejection = redraw, list every legal move, never resend.
2. His LAST move first, then MY destination: 0 guards + his capture = gift.
3. Enemy pawn captures: g6<-f7/h7, b6<-a7/c7, d6<-c7/e7, c5<-b4/d4, e5<-d4/f4, e6<-f7, g5<-h6, f6<-e5. A pawn that MOVED to attack still attacks.
4. Enemy KNIGHT jumps on EVERY destination, the queen's included: d5 owns f4/e3/c3/b4/b6/c7/e7/f6; f6 owns e4/g4/h5/h7/d5/e8/g8/d7. T39R7 21.Qd5?? = Q for N (Nf6 saw d5); T39R2 26.Bf4?? Nxf4.
5. Screens: my piece moving opens a line to my queen or king.
6. A push ends guard duty: name what the pawn left unguarded.
7. Captures: all recapturers, PAWNS first; the defender must LAND on the destination.
8. Rook check: 'can his king just take it?'.
9. Queen hit -> she moves THAT move, off every ray.
10. Down: queens only while safe, no grabs. Up: trade pieces, not pawns.
11. Material FROM THE BOARD after every capture.
12. Clock: book 10 s, routine 15, cap 20; under 3 min 5 s.
13. MATE FIRST, quiet moves too. Keep a rank-1 blocker or luft.
14. SELF-BAN: a move my note called bad is DEAD.
15. Before ANY check: what captures the checker? A check that offers my queen for a rook is a blunder unless forced/mating.
16. Converting material: keep the queen AND every rook defended; a blocker on his bishop's diagonal is a guard - do not move it for free.

## Patterns
- Losses = one-move scan misses (screen / blocked guard / queen on a bishop's or knight's square / piece on a pawn's capture square) + plan drift; the openings are fine.
- A note that NAMES a danger is a self-ban: T38R7 repeated the c4?/Nf4 trap my own Italian note described.
- A fork/attack never excuses skipping the destination scan.
- Winning material is not converting it: after Q-for-R, defend every rook and queen, keep his bishop's diagonal blocked, and refuse unforced queen trades.
