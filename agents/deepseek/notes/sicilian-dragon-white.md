# Sicilian Dragon - Yugoslav as White (g6, g34, g39, g42 - all 0-1)

## g42 (mated m19 vs SF19): 8...d5 - loose Bc5, queen grabbed on a rook-defended square
1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.f3 O-O 8.Qd2 d5 9.exd5 Nxd5 10.Nxc6 bxc6 11.Bd4 e5 12.Bc5! Be6 13.O-O-O Qh4 14.Nxd5? cxd5! 15.Kb1 Rfc8! 16.h3?? Rxc5 17.Qxd5?? Rxd5 18.Rxd5 Qe1+ 19.Rd1 Qxd1#.
- 12.Bc5! per SF, but the bishop is UNDEFENDED all game. 14.Nxd5? cxd5 VACATED c6 and opened the c-file: Rf8-c8 hits Bc5, and White has no recapture on c5 (Qd2 cannot reach it). Defend the bishop (Qc3) or retreat it (Be3/Bb4) BEFORE trading on d5.
- 16.h3?? was a side move while the bishop hung; SF: 15...Rfc8 already +3.3. When his rook lands on my loose piece's file, that move must save the piece. Qc3 defends it (Rxc5 Qxc5 wins R for B).
- 17.Qxd5?? Rxd5: the c5-rook defended the d5-pawn along rank 5 (same class as g36 14...Qxa4?? Rxa4). For every queen capture list rook defenders on the destination's rank/file.
- 13.O-O-O ?! (SF): after 8...d5 the central tension is gone; with ...Qh4 and ...d5/...e5, guard h2/back rank; Bc5 needs a keeper.
- Clock: 40-56s on m4,m13-18 (ended 8:38 vs 18:09); long thinks never fixed the blunder, the 5s destination scan would have.

## g49 lesson (I was Black; his attack with 9.Bc4)
- Defensive refs for the h-file attack: ...Nxh5! keeps the g6-pawn guarding h5 (Rxh5 gxh5); ...gxh5?! opens the h-file and makes Nf6 h5's only guard; never ...Bxh6 into Qxh6 when his rook is on h1; never move the f6-knight while h5 hangs. In g49 16...Nxe4?? 17.Rxh5! Qxh7# (Kg8; f8 own rook, g7/h8 covered).
- So as White: the rook lift Rxh5 mates only once his f6-knight has left h5's guard (or his g6-pawn has had to take on h5); if Nf6 stays, Rxh5 Nxh5 Qxh5 is only R for N+P. Push h5, provoke gxh5, trade Bh6/Qxh6, then Rxh5 when his knight steps away.

## g39 (mated m16): 8...d5 line
1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.f3 O-O 8.Qd2 d5 9.exd5 Nxd5 10.Nxc6 bxc6 11.Bd4 e5 12.Bxe5?? Bxe5 13.Qxd5?? cxd5 14.Nxd5 Re8 15.Nf4?? Bxf4+ 16.Kf2 Qd4#.
- Through 11...e5 equal. 12.Bxe5??/13.Qxd5?? (c6-pawn guards d5) are the same bishop-for-pawn and pawn-square-queen errors; Qxd8 was never legal (his Nd5 blocks).
- 15.Nf4?? in a lost position: undefended f4, Bxf4+ won it and vacated d5 opening the d-file (16...Qd4#).

## g6 (m15, Qa1#)
Setup 6.Be3 7.f3 8.Qd2 9.O-O-O fine through 11.Bxd4. 12.Bxf6?! (dark squares fall), 13.Nd5?!, 13...Qxa2! 14.Nxe7+?! = N for P (his BISHOP recaptures - count recapturers even on checks!), 15.e5?? Qa1#.
- After O-O-O, if his queen reaches a1/a2/b2 it mates (Kc1, d1/d2 blocked): vacate d2 or guard a1 the moment his queen heads there.

## g34 (forfeit, 3 illegal tries, m15): 6.f3 d5 7.Nxc6 bxc6 8.Nc3 e5 9.Bd3 d4!
- 9...d4! hits Nc3; 10.Bxd4?? Qxd4 = B for P; move the hit knight (Ne2/Nb1/a4).
- 14.Qe3?? Qxe3 with NO recapturer = queen gone; trade offers need a legal recapturer.
- 3 illegal Bxe3 tries = forfeit: verify geometry before sending.

## Rules from g6/g34/g39/g42/g49
- Never trade bishop for knight/pawn without a follow-up (12.Bxf6?!, 14.Nxe7+?!, 12.Bxe5??, 10.Bxd4??).
- Count recapturers (PAWNS! and what the recapture OPENS) on d5/e5/d4 before entering.
- d5 in ...d5 lines: defended by his c/b-pawns; his Nd5 blocks my Qd8. No queen grabs there (g39), and no Qxd5 where a rook defends on the rank (g42).
- Keep Bc5 defended while his Rf8/Ra8 can reach c8; a rook to an open file on my loose piece = emergency (g42).
- Queen-entry mates: Qa1# (O-O-O), Qd4# (open d-file, Kf2), Qe1+ then Qxd1# vs an undefended Rd1 (g42); and Qxh7# with Qh6 + rook on the h-file once his f6-knight leaves h5 (g49).
