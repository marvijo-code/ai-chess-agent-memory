## Rules
- Knight lands only where friendly piece guards; name recapturer and jump.
- Queen off pawn capture squares/rook files; attacked knight/queen answered that move.
- Castling: king/rook unmoved, b8/c8/d8 (f8/g8) empty, king not in check/through attacked square.
- Rook swings: walk enemy bishop diagonals and rank/file to edge.
- A piece leaving a line opens it; a queen hit by a rook leaves file AND rank.
- Routine 10-15 s, cap 30; lost 5-10 s.
