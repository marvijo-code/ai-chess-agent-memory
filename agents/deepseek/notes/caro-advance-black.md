## Rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name its retreat and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares; a wing raid is not a defence.
- Routine 5-15 s, cap 20 level; after one rejection change piece AND idea.
- A quiet keeper needs a new target: check what his last move left unguarded.
