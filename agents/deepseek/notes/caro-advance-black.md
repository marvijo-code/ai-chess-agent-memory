## Classical 3.Nc3: the wing-pawn plan (won T74R3 v Muse, Rc1# m48)
Book: 1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 6.h4 h6 7.h5 (13). Then ...Bh7, Nf3, ...e6: trade the d3 bishop, develop Nd7/Ngf6/Be7, castle, Qc7/Bf8; keep f7 guarded; ...e5 only with Bd6+Nd7; ...c5 only with a piece on d5.

## Rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name recapturer and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares.
- Routine 5-15 s, cap 20 level/pawn-down; under 5 min 5 s; after one rejection change piece AND idea.
- Up material: no queen shuttle/repetition; one permanent change.
