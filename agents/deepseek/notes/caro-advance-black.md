## Rules
- A knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name the recapturer and its jump.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, off his rook files; an attacked knight or queen is answered that move.
- Castling legality: king/rook unmoved, b8/c8/d8 (f8/g8) empty, king not in check or through an attacked square.
- Rook swings: walk every enemy bishop diagonal and rank/file to the edge (T68R2 ...d4, T69R4 ...Bxb4).
- Routine 10-15 s, cap 30; lost position 5-10 s.
