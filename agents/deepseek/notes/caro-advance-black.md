3...Bf5 4.Be2 e6 5.Nc3 c5 6.Bb5+ Nc6 7.Bxc6+ bxc6 8.Nge2 Be7. Then take d4, bishop c5/b6, knight e7, castle; c6 guards d5. Plan Bf5-g6, e6, Nd7/c6, c5, Rc8, cxd4.

Build: ...dxe4, ...Bf5; after h4-h5 ...h6/...Bh7; ...Bxd3 Qxd3; ...Nf6, ...e6, ...Be7, O-O, ...Nd5, ...Nxe3, ...Qb6, ...c5. T67R4.2 won with this; T67R3 lost after:
- 17...e5?? kicked a DEFENDED Nd4: 18.Ndf5 hit e7-bishop with tempo, queen lost cover, c-file opened.
- 19...Bxe3?? moved c5-bishop, only unit between Qc3 and Qc7: 20.Qxc7 won queen.
T67R4.2 queen lesson: ...Qb5/...Qb4/...Qa5 chased a knight into c4/b4 tempo; White got Qxb7/Qxa8. When ahead keep queen on safe central square (...Qc7/...Qd6) and guard a8 with ...Rfb8 before she leaves d8. Queen can win her back via ...Qb4+/...Qc7/...Qxa7, but do not allow loose a8 for free.
Down Q for R: 2N v R+pawns is no fortress; his rook/king pick knights, mate m60. Structure moves 5-15 s.
