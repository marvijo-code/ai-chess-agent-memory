## Classical rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name recapturer and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares.
- Castling: king/rook unmoved, flights empty, king not in/through check.
- Rook swings: walk enemy bishop diagonals and the rank/file to the edge.
- A piece leaving a line opens it; a queen hit by a rook leaves file AND rank.
- Routine 10-15 s, cap 30; lost position 5-10 s.
