# Caro-Kann: Advance + Classical 3.Nc3 (B)

## Advance book
1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.e5 Bf5 4.Be2 e6 5.Nc3 c5 6.Bb5+ Nc6 7.Bxc6+ bxc6 8.Nge2 Be7 (16 plies).
Then the pattern: ...Nge7, ...Nf5, ...Bg6, O-O; c6 guards d5; keep the f5 bishop guarded. BANS: no ...f6 without a piece guard on e6; no ...g5/...h5 uncastled; no ...Nh6 while his Bf4/Bg5 lives.
Against 4.Nf3/5.Be2/Nbd2/c3 the same setup is level to move 23. Do not play ...f5 while d5 is guarded only by the queen/king: after ...Bxf5/...exf5 his Qxd5+ wins the pawn (T75SF2G1 24...f5?!). Keep d5 guarded by a piece before any ...f5.

## Classical 3.Nc3 - h4-h5 (won T74R3 v Muse, Rc1# m48)
Book: 1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 6.h4 h6 7.h5 Bh7 8.Nf3 e6 (16 plies).
Then the Black pattern: ...Bxd3 Qxd3, ...Nd7, ...Ndf6, ...Be7, O-O, ...Qc7, ...Bf8; keep f7 guarded; break with ...e5 once ...Bd6 and ...Nd7 stand behind it (...e5 won a pawn: dxe5 Bxe5 hits the queen). ...c5 only with a piece guard on d5.
Conversion vs a long-castled king: ...b5, ...c5-c4-c3 clamp, ...Rc4, ...Nf4 (covers e2/g2), ...h4, Rc1#.

## Rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name the recapturer and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares.
- Castling: K/R unmoved, flights empty, not through check; count HIS rook's file before long castling.
- Routine 5-15 s, cap 20 in a level or pawn-down position; after one rejection change piece AND idea.
