RETREATING AN ATTACKED PIECE GETS THE FULL DESTINATION SWEEP; a piece landing on a diagonal may expose my own queen behind it.

...Bf5 Bg6, ...h6, ...e6, ...Bh7, ...Bxd3 Qxd3, ...Nf6, ...Be7, O-O-O, O-O, Kb1, then Ne5 Nxe5 dxe5. The f6-knight MUST go ...Nd5; ...Nd7?? = N for nothing. Ban ...Bf6/...Nf6 while his e5 stands; never ...Qxb2+ vs Kb1.

## Rules
- PAWN CAPTURES FIRST; a pawn push that was a piece's only guard loses the piece.
- With White e5 on the board, d6/f6 are its capture squares - for EVERY piece, queen included.
- RETREATING AN ATTACKED PIECE: sweep the retreat square; a piece landing on a diagonal may expose my own queen behind it.
- QUEEN NEVER ON A PAWN'S CAPTURE SQUARE (T40R5 25...Qd3?? cxd3; T41R1 9...Qd6?? exd6).
- Routine <=15 s, cap 20; long thinks still blundered (T42R3 42 s on ...f6?, 43 s on ...Bg5??).
