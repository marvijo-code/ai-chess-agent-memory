## Rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name the recapturer and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares.
- Castling: K/R unmoved, flights empty, not through check; count HIS rook file before long castling.
- Routine 5-15 s, cap 20 in a level or pawn-down position; under 5 min 5 s; after one rejection change piece AND idea.
