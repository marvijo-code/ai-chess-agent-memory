# Caro-Kann Advance + Classical 3.Nc3 (B)

## Advance book
3...Bf5 4.Be2 e6 5.Nc3 c5 6.Bb5+ Nc6 7.Bxc6+ bxc6 8.Nge2 Be7. Then take on d4, bishop c5/b6, knight e7, castle; the c6-pawn guards d5. Plan: bishop f5 then g6, pawn e6, knight d7/c6, pawn c5, rook c8, then take on d4.
BANS: queen takes d4, queen d6, bishop g5, queen takes b2; with White's e5-pawn a knight to f6 throws a knight; no piece on d6/f6; no knight c5 while d4/b4; vs b3/Ba3 Bxa3. If Bf5 is traded while Ng8 is home, do not castle.

## T62Final v SF19 (0-1 m34) - Qb4?? cxb4; passive Nb8
Book to 12...Bc5 13.Qd4 Ba5 14.b3 Ne7 15.c3 O-O 16.h4. 16...Bb6?! gave bishop for knight (Bb6 was needed on c5); 17...Nc8?? was the game-loser (+0.13 -> +8.58): a quiet knight reroute to c8 where it defends nothing, so 18.Na4 and White won material. Finishing blunder 21...Qb4?? cxb4 - my queen on White's c3-pawn's capture square, Q for a pawn. LESSONS: (a) never a queen on an enemy pawn's capture square - sweep the pawn map before EVERY queen move, quiet ones included; (b) when a plan stalls do NOT reroute a knight to a passive square - keep it active or trade it. 4 illegal tries (O-O twice when castling was illegal, plus a phantom axb6).

## 4.Be2 / 4.Nf3 and the f5 break
3.e5 Bf5 4.Be2 e6 5.Nc3 Nd7 6.Nf3 Ne7 7.Nh4 Bg6 8.O-O Nf5 (12 plies). 6...Ngf6?? 7.exf6 loses a KNIGHT. Take on g6 with ...hxg6, never ...Nxg6.
T56R3: 13.g4 c5?! 14.f5! exf5 15.gxf5 Bxf5?? 16.Rxf5 - that capture removed e6, f5's only guard. Retreat the bishop BEFORE f5, or meet f5 with ...f6.

## Classical 3.Nc3 (B)
Bishop f5 then g6 after h6; pawn e6; bishop h7; trade on d3 and recapture with the queen; knight f6; bishop e7; castle; Kb1; later Ne5. The f6-knight goes to d5 - a knight to d7 is a knight for nothing. Ban ...Bf6 and ...Nf6 while his e5 stands.

## Old patterns
- Knight e7-f5 covers g7 BEFORE ...Be7; vs 7...Be7?! 8.Qg4! g7 is king-guarded only: 8...h5?? 9.Qxg7. Cover g7 with a PIECE.
- T42R6 11...Nxe5??: f5 does NOT attack e5 and his Re1 owned the e-file - 12.Rxe5. Write the landing square, its guards and the recapturer's real jump.
- vs 5.Nxd4/6.Nxc6 plan: bxc6, e6, Ne7-g6, castle, f6; 15...Bf6?? loses the bishop to exf6.

## Rules
- PAWN CAPTURES FIRST; a push that was a piece's only guard loses the piece.
- With White's e5 on the board d6/f6 are its squares for EVERY piece, queen included.
- KNIGHT CAPTURES LAND ONLY WHERE A FRIENDLY PIECE GUARDS; name the recapturer and its jump.
- QUEEN OFF PAWN CAPTURE SQUARES AND OFF THE FILE HIS ROOK CAN ENTER.
- An attacked knight must be answered that move; do not take e6 with the f-pawn while his rook owns the e-file.
- CASTLING legality: king/rook unmoved, f8/g8 empty, king not in check and not passing through an attacked square.
- Routine <=15 s, cap 30; long thinks blundered anyway.
