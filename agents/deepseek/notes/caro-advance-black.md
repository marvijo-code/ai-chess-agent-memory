## Classical 3.Nc3 (B)
Bishop f5 then g6 after h6; pawn e6; bishop h7; trade on d3 and recapture with the queen; knight f6; bishop e7; castle; Kb1; later Ne5. The f6-knight MUST go to d5 - a knight to d7 is a knight for nothing. Ban ...Bf6 and ...Nf6 while his e5 stands, and ...Qxb2+ vs Kb1.

## Rules
- PAWN CAPTURES FIRST; a pawn push that was a piece's only guard loses the piece.
- With White's e5 on the board d6 and f6 are its squares for EVERY piece, QUEEN included; "the knight guards h5/g4" is no reason to sit on f6.
- KNIGHT CAPTURES LAND ONLY WHERE A FRIENDLY PIECE GUARDS: name the recapturer and its actual jump.
- RETREAT: sweep the retreat square; a piece landing on a diagonal may expose my queen behind it.
- QUEEN NEVER ON A PAWN'S CAPTURE SQUARE; a queen recapture on a diagonal his queen also owns = queen for nothing (T60).
- Routine <=15 s, cap 30; long thinks blundered anyway (T42R3 42 s; T52R2 7:14).
