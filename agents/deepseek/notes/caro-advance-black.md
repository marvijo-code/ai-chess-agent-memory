## Classical 3.Nc3 - h4-h5 (WON T74R3 v Muse, Rc1# m48)
1.e4 c6 2.d4 d5 3.Nc3 dxe4 4.Nxe4 Bf5 5.Ng3 Bg6 6.h4 h6 7.h5 Bh7 (14 plies). Then ...e6, ...Bxd3, ...Nf6, ...Be7, O-O, ...Nbd7, ...Re8, ...Qc7, ...Bd6; ...c5 only with a piece guard on d5.
- With ...Nd7 guarding e5 behind ...Bd6, the ...e5 break won a pawn: dxe5 Bxe5, my knight landing on e5 hits his queen. Then ...Bxg3 wrecked his pawns and ...Be5/...Bxd4 simplified to +N.
- CONVERSION vs a long-castled king: ...b5, then ...c5-c4-c3 clamp, ...Rc4, ...Nf4 (covers e2/g2, blocks f3), ...h4, Rc1#. Do NOT shuttle ...Nd5/...Nf6 (four flips, 8 min, no gain) - push the clamp pawns.
- T72S2G1 v Muse: same ...c5-c4 clamp, ...b5-a5-b4, ...bxc3+ Nxc3 Rxc3, ...Rxc2+ won N+B.
- T72R5 v SF19: ...f6 with ...Bf8? left the f5-bishop loose; with the king on e8/d8 keep e6+f6 intact and no break before castling.

## Rules
- Knight lands only where a friendly piece guards; name the recapturer and his pawn captures.
- Queen off pawn capture squares, rook files and knight-check squares.
- Castling: K/R unmoved, flights empty, not through check; with queens on count HIS rook's file before long castling.
- Routine 10-15 s, cap 30; level or worse after m25 cap 15 s. Illegal tries come in pairs (2 in T74R3): after one rejection change piece AND idea.
