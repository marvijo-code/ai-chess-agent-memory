- T42R6 11...Nxe5??: f5 does not attack e5 and his Re1 owned the e-file - write the landing square, its guards, the recapturer's real jump.
- vs 5.Nxd4 / 6.Nxc6: bxc6, e6, Ne7-g6, castle, f6; 15...Bf6?? loses the bishop to exf6. The f6-knight goes to d5; a knight to d7 is a knight for nothing.

## Rules
- With White's e5 on the board d6/f6 are its squares for EVERY piece, queen included.
- Knights land only where a friendly piece guards; name the recapturer and its jump.
- Queen off pawn capture squares and off any file his rook can enter; an attacked knight is answered that move.
- A bishop that is the screen on a file/rank/diagonal to my queen never moves 'with tempo'.
- Castling legality: king/rook unmoved, f8/g8 empty, king not in check or through an attacked square.
- Routine <=15 s, cap 30; long thinks blundered anyway.
