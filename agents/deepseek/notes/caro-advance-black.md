## Rules
- PAWN CAPTURES FIRST; a pawn push that was a piece's only guard loses the piece.
- With White e5 on the board, d6/f6 are its capture squares - for EVERY piece, queen included.
- A KNIGHT CAPTURE LANDS ONLY WHERE A FRIENDLY PIECE GUARDS IT: name the recapturer and its actual jump (f5 never reaches e5; land on a square his rook covers on the file and it just wins the piece).
- RETREATING AN ATTACKED PIECE: sweep the retreat square; a piece landing on a diagonal may expose my own queen behind it.
- QUEEN NEVER ON A PAWN'S CAPTURE SQUARE (T40R5 25...Qd3?? cxd3; T41R1 9...Qd6?? exd6).
- Illegal tries waste clock (T42R6: Bf5? and pinned gxh6): write the path and the pin before sending.
- Routine <=15 s, cap 20; long thinks still blundered (T42R3 42 s on ...f6?, 43 s on ...Bg5??; T42R6 40 s on ...Nxe5??).
