# QGD as White (Lasker 0-8; Bxf6 h6 0-2; Exchange 0-1; Tartakower 0-1)
The openings keep giving playable positions; every loss is one middle-game discipline miss.

## Pawn-diagonal rule (g131, g133, g136)
- Before EVERY queen move list each enemy pawn's capture squares: e6 hits d5/f5, d5 hits c4/e4, f7 hits e6/g6.
- 3 queen-for-pawn losses: g131 20.Qxf5?? exf5; g133 15.Qe4?? dxe4; g136 27.Qd5?? exd5. 'Defended' and 'he might not' never save.
- With d5 standing c4/e4 are death for any piece; with e6 standing d5/f5 are.

## Bxf6 h6 (0-2)
- After ...cxd4/...dxc4/...b6/...Bb7 d4 is a target, not a gift; play Ne4/Bb5/Ne5; never a piece on d5 (e6-pawn) or d6 (Qd7); never Q-grab on d4 while his queen+bishop hold it.
- g113 (m19): 14.Nd5?? cxd5; 18.Qc4 Qg5! threatens Qxg2# (play f3). g114 (m27): 14...Nxd4!; 16.Bd5?? exd5; 21.Nd6?? Qxd6.

## General
- His Qe7-b4/h4 swings hit pins and loose pawns: cover b2/a2 before Be2/O-O.
- Queen hit -> she moves that move, to a square no pawn, knight or ray touches; 'defended' never counts.
- Count attackers vs defenders before any capture (2 vs 3 = no); equal = HOLD; no piece-for-pawn; down: hold, trade pawns, repetition.
- Routine <=15 s; book <=10 s; 34-70 s thinks still blundered (g133,g136).
