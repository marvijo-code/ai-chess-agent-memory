- g111 (0-1 m39): 13.h3 Bxf3 14.Qxf3 Rad8 15.Rfe1 Rfe8 16.Rad1 Qd6 17.e4 dxe4 18.Bxe4 Qf6 19.Qxf6 gxf6 20.Bxc6 bxc6. Errors:

  - 26.Rxc6?? Rxc6 = R for P (his c4-rook defended c6; a recapture is not a trade). Down a pawn an R-ending draws; R for P loses. 34...Qa2+ 35...Rxf2 36...Rg2+ mate (Q+R rank 2 battery).
  - Clock: 37-47 s on book moves; 8:45 vs 14:24; book <=10 s.
- g108: 13.Be2 Rd8 14.O-O Bd7 15.Rc1 Rac8 16.Qb3 Na5 17.Qc2 Nc6 18.Rfd1 Qf6 19.Bd3 Kh8 20.Ne5? Nxe5 21.dxe5 Qxe5 (drops e5). 22.Qb3?? leaves b7 to Bc6: 23.Qxb7?? Bxb7 = Q for P. NEVER Qx b7/d7/a8 with a c6-bishop (g105 too).

- g102: 21.Nxc6?? Bxc6 = N for one pawn; 26.Qd3?? Bxd3 - queen back on the line that chased her.
- g98: 13.Be2? Qb4! pins Nd2 and hits b2 -> Qxb2. With Nd2+Ke1+Qe7, ...Qb4 is always in the air: Qc2 or Qb3 before Be2/O-O.

## Bxf6 h6 (g113, 0-1 vs SF m19)
5.Bg5 h6 6.Bxf6 Bxf6 7.e3 c6 8.Bd3 Nd7 9.O-O O-O 10.Rc1 Re8 11.Qe2 e5 12.dxe5 dxc4 13.Bxc4 Bxe5 = (-0.06). Evals fine, then:
- 14.Nd5?? cxd5 (piece for pawn, +5): d5 is guarded by the c6-pawn. Nc3 was hit by Be5, but the escape must be pawn-safe: 14.Ne2/Nb1. A 'strong central square' his pawn covers is death.
- 18.Qc4 Qg5! threatens Qxg2#: his Bb7 (c6/d5/e4/f3 clear) guards g2; play 19.f3. 19.Qc7?? Qxg2#. When down, scan HIS mate nets before any material grab.

## Tartakower g96 (0-1) - condensed
5.Bg5 h6 7.Bh4 b6 8.Bd3 Bb7 9.O-O Nbd7 10.Rc1 c5 11.cxd5 exd5 12.Bg3 Rc8 13.Ne5 Nxe5 14.Bxe5 Bd6 15.Bxd6 Qxd6 16.dxc5 bxc5 =. NEVER Bg6?? (fxg6); never Qd5+ beside Qd6.

## General rules
- His Qe7-b4/h4 swings hit pins and loose pawns: cover b2/a2 before Be2/O-O; don't castle while Nd2 can be pinned.
- A pawn push abandons the square it guarded (c4 -> d4; g111).
- Down: hold, trade pawns, activate king; no piece onto a defended square; no 'regain' grabs; repetition.
- Equal = HOLD: every loss was middle-game discipline, not the opening. Routine <=15 s.
