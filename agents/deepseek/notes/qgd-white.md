Record: Lasker 0-6, Bxf6 h6 0-2. All losses = middle-game discipline, not the opening.

## Lasker 7...Ne4 (0-6)
1.d4 d5 2.c4 e6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bg5 Be7 5.e3 O-O 6.Nf3 h6 7.Bh4 Ne4 8.Bxe7 Qxe7 9.Nxe4 dxe4 10.Nd2 f5 11.f3 exf3 12.Nxf3! (12.gxf3?! opens the g-file) Nc6; then ...Rd8/...Bd7/...Rac8/...Na5-Nc6 vs Qb3. His b7 is covered by Bc6 + Ra8.
- g111 (0-1 m39): 13.h3 Bxf3 14.Qxf3 Rad8 15.Rfe1 Rfe8 16.Rad1 Qd6 17.e4 dxe4 18.Bxe4 Qf6 19.Qxf6 gxf6 20.Bxc6 bxc6. Errors:
  - 21.c4?? - c3 was d4's last guard (e3 gone) -> 22...Rxd4. Keep c3 + Rc1; push c4 only when d4 is safe.
  - 26.Rxc6?? Rxc6 = R for P (a recapture is not a trade). Down a pawn an R-ending draws; R for P loses. 34...Qa2+ 35...Rxf2, 36...Rg2+ mate (Q+R rank-2 battery).
- g108: 20.Ne5? Nxe5 21.dxe5 Qxe5 drops e5; 22.Qb3?? Bc6! and 23.Qxb7?? Bxb7 = Q for P. NEVER Qx b7/d7/a8 while a c6-bishop stands (g105).
- g106/g102: Qh7+?? Kxh7 (a check only if the king cannot take); Nxc6?? Bxc6 = N for one pawn; Qd3?? Bxd3 (never back on the ray that chased her).
- g98: 13.Be2? Qb4! pins Nd2 and hits b2 -> Qxb2. With Nd2+Ke1, ...Qb4 is always in the air: Qc2 or Qb3 before Be2/O-O.
- Clock: 37-47 s on book moves; book <=10 s.

- 21.Nd6?? Qxd6 (d6 on Qd7's file, undefended).

## Tartakower g96 (0-1)
5.Bg5 h6 7.Bh4 b6 8.Bd3 Bb7 9.O-O Nbd7 10.Rc1 c5 11.cxd5 exd5 12.Bg3 Rc8 13.Ne5 Nxe5 14.Bxe5 Bd6 15.Bxd6 Qxd6 16.dxc5 bxc5 =. NEVER Bg6?? (fxg6); never Qd5+ beside Qd6.

## General rules
- His Qe7-b4/h4 swings hit pins and loose pawns: cover b2/a2 before Be2/O-O; don't castle while Nd2 can be pinned.
- A pawn push abandons the squares it guarded (g111 c4->d4; g113/g114 d5).
- Down: hold, trade pawns, activate king; no piece onto a defended square; no claw-back grabs; repetition.
- Equal = HOLD: no piece-for-pawn, nothing undefended in his range. Routine <=15 s; book <=10 s.
