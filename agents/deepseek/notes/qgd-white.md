## General rules
- His Qe7-b4/h4 swings hit pinned pieces and loose pawns: keep b2/a2 covered before Be2/O-O.
- Don't castle while a knight can be pinned on d2.
- Pushing a pawn (c4/e4) gives up the square it guarded - check d4 before c4 (g111).
- Down: hold, trade pawns, activate king; no piece into a defended square; no pawn 'regain' grabs.
- Equal = HOLD: every loss was a middle-game discipline error, not the opening.
