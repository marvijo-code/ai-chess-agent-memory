## g98 vs Sonnet (0-1, Rg4# m32) - ...Qb4! hits b2 through the pinned Nd2
13.Be2? Qb4! (pins Nd2 to Ke1 AND hits undefended b2) 14.O-O? Qxb2 15.Rb1 Qxa2 16.Qb3?? Qxb3 17.Nxb3 b6 = two pawns down.
- With Nd2 alive, Ke1 and Qe7 on the board, ...Qb4 is always in the air: play Qc2 or offer the Qb3 trade BEFORE Be2/O-O. Never castle into the pin.
- Endgame after 17.Nxb3: 20.Nd7?? Bxd7 - d7 was defended by Bc8 and both rooks were defended: the 'family fork' never existed. Down pawns: hold, trade pawns, no pseudo-activity.

## General QGD-White rules
- In queen endings his Qe7-b4/h4 swings hit pinned pieces and loose pawns: keep b2/a2 covered (Qc2, Rb1) before Be2/O-O.
- Don't castle while a knight can be pinned on d2; resolve the pin or defend the pawn he attacks.
- Down pawns in symmetric endgames: hold, trade pawns, activate king; no piece into a defended square.
- 0-3 in the QGD; the opening keeps reaching = positions, so the fix is middle-game discipline (queen lines, material counts), not the opening choice.
