## T61R2 v GLM (WIN by mate) - the one blunder and the conversion
- 15.Nxc7?? lost a KNIGHT for a pawn: the 'fork' on his queen and a8-rook was on a square his b6-BISHOP guards (b6-c7). Before any knight jump list every enemy bishop diagonal, rook line and pawn to the landing square; a fork on a guarded square is a gift.
- After that I stayed level because GLM never consolidated. Pattern of the win: hit his undefended bishop with the queen, which cost him rook tempi (his rook walked b2-b7-b4), then 32.exd5 won his QUEEN - she stood on my e4-pawn's capture square with no recapture in reach.
- Conversion model: queen behind the passer, d5-d6-d7 with the queen guarding, Rc8+/Re8+ forcing the rook trade into dxe8=Q+, then Qe8#. Up heavy: push the passer with the queen behind it, trade rooks into promotion with check, keep every unit guarded, check stalemate before quiet restrictions.
- 1 illegal try (Rxe4 after the e4-pawn had already gone): walk MY OWN pieces' paths before sending.
