## Tournament 3, round 3: repetition draw
10...Bd6 11.d4?! Qh4! 12.g3 Qxd4! 13.Bb3 Bg4 14.Be3 Bxf3 15.Bxd4! c5 16.Be3! Rfe8 17.Bc4 Rad8 18.Rae1 b6 19.Bg5 Rxe1 20.Rxe1! Rf8 21.Re3 Bb7 22.Be7 Bxe7 23.Rxe7! Rd8 24.f4 Rd1+?! 25.Kf2! Rd2+? 26.Ke1 h6?! 27.Kxd2.

### Pawn gain, then hanging rook
- Bd6 preserved the bishop without the earlier ...Be6/...fxe6 structure. Qh4 threatened Qxh2# with Bd6 support; g3 answered the threat but allowed Qxd4, taking the undefended pawn and attacking Bc4. Qh4 and Qxd4 were marked only good moves.
- Bg4 attacked Qf3. After Be3 attacked Qd4, Bxf3 Bxd4 exchanged queens through the bishops and retained the extra pawn. This establishes the material outcome, not that every earlier move was best.
- After 23.Rxe7, White had Kg1/Re7/Bc4 and pawns a2 b2 c2 f2 g3 h2. Black had Kg8/Rf8/Bb7 and pawns a7 b6 c7 c5 f7 g7 h7.
- Rd8 threatened Rd1 mate while f2 blocked the king's escape. White's f4 opened f2; Rd1+ Kf2 therefore did not force mate.
- Rd2+ was undefended. Ke1 escaped check and attacked d2 directly. Black had to assess that capture before choosing another move.
- h6 created h7 as an escape against Re8#, but left Kxd2 available. Solving one threat did not solve the rook's safety. No engine-approved replacement was supplied.
- Black still had 10:54 after h6 and spent 44 seconds on it. The failure was candidate verification, not time pressure.

### Practical resistance and limits of the draw
After losing the rook, White collected f7/g7. Black advanced the queenside majority: ...a6, ...b5, ...c4, ...c5, ...a5, ...b4. Exchanges produced a c-pawn that reached c2, supported by Bb3; ...a4 protected the bishop. The active king captured White's kingside pawns.

57.Bxc2 Kf2 58.Rxb3 axb3 59.Bxb3 left White king, light-square bishop and a-pawn against king. The promotion square a8 is light: this was not the wrong-bishop rook-pawn exception. White subsequently repeated bishop/king maneuvers while Black blocked with ...Ka5/...Ka6. The recorded threefold draw demonstrates practical resistance against this depth-4 ladder, not a theoretically drawn ending or soundness of the rook sacrifice. Black finished with 6:45 and no illegal attempts.
