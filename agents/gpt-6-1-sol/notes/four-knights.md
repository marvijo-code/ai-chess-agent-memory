# Four Knights: loose pieces and defensive priorities

## Tournament 2, round 3: Black vs Stockfish 19, checkmate loss
1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bb5 Bb4 5.O-O O-O 6.Nd5 Nxd5 7.exd5 e4 8.dxc6 exf3 9.Qxf3 dxc6 10.Bc4 Be6 11.Bxe6 fxe6! 12.Qb3 Qd5?? 13.Qxb4.

### Decisive bishop loss
- The central sequence exchanged both pairs of knights. After Bxe6 fxe6, Black's remaining bishop was on b4, attacked by Qb3.
- I claimed ...Qd5 defended Bb4 and e6 while offering a queen exchange. It defended e6 along d5-e6, but d5 and b4 are not aligned: the bishop remained loose.
- Qd5 attacked Qb3 through c4. White could ignore that attack by capturing Bb4, moving the queen to safety while winning a piece.
- An offered Qxd5 exchange was not forced. Calculate the opponent's strongest capture, not only the cooperative exchange that repairs my pawns.
- No best replacement for 12...Qd5 was supplied. Establish actual protection or a safe bishop retreat before pursuing a queen exchange.

### Activity did not repair the deficit
13...a5 14.Qa3 Rf5 15.d3 Raf8 16.d4 Qxd4 17.Qg3 R5f6 18.c3 Qd5 19.f4 Rg6 20.Qh3 Rh6 21.Qf3 Qf5 22.a4 e5 23.Qe2 exf4 24.Qc4+ Kh8 25.Rxf4 Qf6 26.b3 Qe7 27.Rxf8+ Qxf8 28.Bxh6 gxh6.

- Rook lifts attacked the queen, but White retained the extra bishop until exchanging it for Rh6.
- After 28...gxh6, White had Q+R against Q, with five pawns against six. My earlier bishop deficit had become a rook deficit. One extra pawn was not material balance.
- After each exchange, reconstruct the surviving pieces and pawns. Do not carry forward an obsolete description such as 'White's extra bishop.'

### Final mating threat
After further queen checks:
32.g3 Qe7 33.Rd1 c5 34.Qg4+ Kh8 35.Qc8+ Kg7 36.Qg4+ Kh8 37.Rf1 Qe3+ 38.Kh1 Qxc3?? 39.Rf8#.

- White's quiet Rf1 prepared entry to f8. My queen check did not remove that threat.
- After Kh1, enumerate White's next checks before collecting c3. Rf8 checked along the eighth rank; Qg4 controlled g8 and g7, and my h7 pawn blocked h7.
- Qxc3 neither checked nor defended f8. A concrete defensive resource was 38...Qe7, meeting Rf8+ with Qxf8; this stops the immediate mate but does not establish a drawable position.
- I had 9:43 after Qxc3. The loss came from missing the threat with ample time, not from time pressure.

Only fxe6 and the first Qd5 received supplied marks. The final Qxc3 error is independently established by the immediate mate; unmarked rook maneuvers are not certified sound.
