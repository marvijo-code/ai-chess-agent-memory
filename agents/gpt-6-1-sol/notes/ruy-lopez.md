## T8: Black vs Sonnet
14.d5 Nd8?! ...g6?! ...Bf8? ...Bg7? repeated an unproven defensive scheme.
- With Rc1/Qc7 screened by Nc5 and Bc2, ...Na4? Bxa4 bxa4 Rxc7 removed both screens and lost queen for rook. Pawn protection of Na4 did not preserve Qc7.
- ...Bf6 landed on g5's capture square. After gxf6, Bd4 controlled f6 through empty e5, making ...Kxf6 illegal. The new f6 pawn anchored Qe7+/Qg7#.

## T7 and earlier recurring errors
- Be3?! Nxd4! Nxd4? exd4 Qxd4 Qxc2: central liquidation opened the c-file and Qxd4 abandoned Bc2.
- ...Qc4?? Rxc4 Rxc4 won queen for rook; Qxc4 instead merely exchanges queens. A defended queen can lose to a cheaper piece.
- Qg3 threatened Qxg7# but abandoned Bd4 to Rxd4. ...Rg4 later attacked the queen but allowed Qh7#, protected by Rf7 through g7.
- Nxc5 dxc5 opened Bc8-d7-e6-f5; Nf5 allowed the OTHER bishop's Bxf5. exf5 removed e4's support of d5.
- Bd3 cleared Rc1 against Qc7; ...Nxb4 ignored Rxc7. Qa4 lost to bxa4; Nb3 overlooked axb3.
- Nh4-f5 Bxf5 Ng3xf5, f3-f4, and Re3-g3 remove e4 defenders. Be7 can block Re8's apparent defense of e5.
- Game 4 won material through discovered checks but flagged with queen against bare king. Execute elementary mates promptly.
