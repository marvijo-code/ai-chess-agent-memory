## Other geometry
- Nc3 does not attack d4. Qe2 can abandon d4; recount central guards.
- ...e3 clears Bb7-g2: an attack on Q does not answer Qxg2#.
- A screen can cover two rays: Nc3 screened Qa5-e1 and Rc5-c2; Ne4 allowed Qxe1+ first.
- Bd3 blocks Rd1-d5; Bxg6 clears it. R6d3 blocks Qe2-b5, abandoning Nb5.
- A bishop recapture on d5 can clear the e-file and allow Rxe7+ to win Q; a rook recapture may preserve the bishop screen. Compare recapturers.
- A pinned rook can capture its pinning queen: Qxa7 permits Rxa7.
