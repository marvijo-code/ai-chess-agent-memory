# Ruy Lopez: screens, restriction and conversion

## T18 round 3: White vs Sonnet, mate win
Chigorin: 12...cxd4 13.cxd4! Nc6 14.Nf1 Bd7 15.Ng3 Rac8 16.Be3?! Rfe8? 17.Rc1 g6 18.d5 Nb4? 19.Bb1?! Qd8 20.a3 Nbxd5 21.exd5 Nxd5 22.Bd2?! Rxc1 23.Bxc1 Bf6?! 24.Qxd5.
- After a3, Nb4's exits were restricted: a6 occupied by Black's pawn; c6 attacked by d5; a2/c2/d3 controlled by Bb1, with Rc1 also controlling c2. ...Nbxd5 exd5 Nxd5 surrendered a knight for two pawns. This is concrete geometry, not a verified forced opening trap: Be3, Bb1 and Bd2 received adverse marks; no best replacements were supplied.
- Nxd5 attacked Be3. Bd2 saved it but was inaccurate. After ...Rxc1, Bxc1 vacated d2 and restored Qd1-d2-d3-d4-d5. ...Bf6 left Nd5 undefended, allowing Qxd5. Compare recapturers for the lines they restore as well as the guards they preserve.
- Qxd5 Be6 attacked the queen: Qd1 preserved the gain. Ng5 Bxg5 Bxg5 traded a knight for a bishop while retaining two extra minors for two pawns.

28.Be3 d5 29.Ba2 f5 30.f4 e4 31.Bd4 Bf7 32.Ne2 Rc8 33.Nc3 Qe6 34.Bxd5 Qd7 35.Bxf7+ Qxf7 36.Be3 Qe6 37.Nd5 h6 38.Qd4 Qc6 39.Ne7+ Kh7 40.Nxc6 Rxc6 41.Qd7+ Kg8 42.Qxc6.
- ...f5 threatened ...f4, forking Be3/Ng3. f4 prevented that advance; Bd4 blockaded d5, and Ng3-e2-c3 attacked d5/e4. Extra pieces should restrain connected passers before collecting more material.
- Ba2 did NOT initially pin Be6 to Kg8: Black's d5 pawn blocked a2-b3-c4-d5-e6-f7-g8. The game commentary overstated the pin. Trace the complete diagonal.
- Bxd5 from a2 removed the central pawn with an attack on Qe6; Nc3 protected the bishop. Qe6 also screened Bf7's diagonal to d5. Bxf7+ then exchanged Black's last bishop; Be3 cleared d4 so Qd1 could defend Nd5.
- Qc6 avoided the anticipated Nf6+ but allowed Ne7+, which attacked Kg8, Qc6 and Rc8. Enumerate ALL checking knight jumps, not just the first candidate. Nxc6 won Q for N; Qd7+ subsequently checked Kh7 and attacked Rc6.
- Finish 43.Bd4+ Kh7 44.Qd7+ Kg8 45.Qg7#. Bd4 protected g7 and controlled h8; Black's own g6/h6 pawns restricted exits.
- No invalid attempts; finished with 8:11. Routine opening moves took 3-16 seconds, later decisions often 20-45. The win came from material gains and forks; it does not validate the marked opening inaccuracies.

## T17 semifinal: White vs Sonnet
17.d5 Nb4 18.Bb1 Rfc8 19.a3 Nc2 20.Bxc2 Qxc2 21.Qxc2 Rxc2 22.Rab1 b4 23.axb4 a3 24.bxa3 Rxa3.
- Bb1 retained e4's defense; Rab1 guarded b2. Queen exchanges did not eliminate second-rank rook threats. Black's attempted ...Bxb4 was illegal because its own d6 pawn blocked Be7-b4.
- Rec1? and f3?! remain warnings. ...Ra7 lost R to Be3-a7 through d4/c5/b6; Rc8 pinned Bd8, and ...Kf8 allowed Rxd8+.
- Rc7 supported b7. ...Kd8 unpinned Nd7, which could capture on b8; Rxd7+ Kxd7 b8=Q removed that guard before promotion.

## Other Chigorin lessons
- ...a5 frees a6 for Nb4-a6-c5; d6 supports Nc5. Be3 screens Re1: recount e4 when Bb1 leaves. Rc1/Re1 allow ...Nd3 forks unless d3 is guarded.
- T17 Black: ...dxc5/...c4 made a protected passer; Bd6 blockaded d5. With Kh2: ...f5 exf5?? e4+ cleared d6-e5-f4-g3-h2 and hit Nf3. The earlier pawn pushes were inaccurate despite later gains.
- ...Rc2?? was unsound: Qxc2 preserved Ne3's screen on Qb6-f2; Nxc2?? Qxf2+ lost that screen.
- ...a4 b4 allows ...axb3 e.p. Bxb3 abandons e4; axb3 can open Ra8-a1 while Bb1 blocks Re1's recapture. Nd2 can block Qe2's defense of Bc2.
- Nxc4 can abandon Rf7 to Kxf7. Save threatened material before recapturing; support passers with king/minors.
