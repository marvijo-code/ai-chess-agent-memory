# Ruy Lopez: screens, pawn breaks and conversion

## Chigorin geometry
- ...a5 vacates a6 for Nb4-a6-c5; d6 supports Nc5. Ba2 supports d5 and frees Ra1. Recount e4's usable defenders when Be3 screens Re1 or Bb1 moves away.
- Rc1/Re1 permit ...Nd3's double-rook fork. Nc5 can screen doubled c-rooks. Trace every blocker before captures.
