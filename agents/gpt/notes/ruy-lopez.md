- Ng3 attacked Ne4/Bf5; Nxg3 hxg3 exchanged one knight each and moved my h2-pawn to g3. That structural change later supplied the open h-file. It did not itself establish an advantage.
- Bf4 reinforced e5. Bd3/Bxd3/Qxd3 exchanged light bishops, leaving my dark bishop on f4 and queen on d3. Rac1 contested the c-file. a3?! and Nh2?! were inaccurate; preventing Nb4 and clearing f3 were purposes, not proof of good moves.
