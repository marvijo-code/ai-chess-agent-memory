# Ruy Lopez: clearance, screens and conversion

## T24 round 3: White vs Sonnet, mate win
Berlin 4.d3 Bc5; c3/h3/Re1/Bc2/d4, ...exd4 cxd4! Bb4 Nc3 Bxc3 bxc3! Re8 Bf4 Qe7 e5 dxe5 dxe5 Nh5 Bh2 Rad8 Qd3?! f5? exf6 e.p. Nxf6 Rxe7 Rxe7.
- cxd4 and bxc3 were sole good moves. e5 cleared Bc2-h7 and attacked Nf6. Qd3 was marked inaccurate: the battery did not establish a forced advantage.
- En passant removed e5 AND f5, opening Re1-e7 against Qe7. Nxf6 recovered the pawn but left the queen exposed. Calculate every line cleared by en passant, including the departing pawn's file.
- Nd4 Nxd4 cxd4 simplified, but d5 Bxd5 surrendered the passer: Nf6 defended d5, so Qxd5 would lose Q. A passed pawn is not automatically safe to advance.
- Bf5 Bxf5 Qxf5 and Rxd8+ Rxd8 reduced counterplay. Before the rook trade I identified the back-rank danger with Bh2 occupying the escape square. Bxc7 freed h2 with tempo on Rd8; this later answered ...Rd1+.
- ...Rd7 Be5 Rd5 skewered Be5/Qf5. Qe6+ moved Q with tempo and defended B; Bxf6 Rd1+ Kh2 gxf6 Qxf6 reached Q versus R. Check opponent checks before claiming immediate liquidation.
- Qg4 abandoned f2's defense, allowing ...Rxf2. Later Kg3 Rg7+ Kf4?! abandoned g2; ...Rxg2! took it. King activation must account for guards lost, even with a queen advantage.
- Active king route Kf5-e6-e7-f6-f7 restricted Black. After Kf7 Rg7+, Qxg7# worked because Kf7 protected Qg7: Kh7 could NOT recapture. Refresh king support before assuming a rook protected by its king is immune.
- No invalid attempts; finished 9:30. Several 35-46-second conversion moves still missed pawn losses. Use time for concrete defenses, checks and rook forks.
