## T39 round 2: White Maroczy vs SF19, mate loss
- c4/Nc3 met Qa5; f3 supplied e4's legal guard. Qxd4 was required after Nxd4. Qd2/Be2/O-O preserved material and avoided earlier long-castling exposure.
- With b3 played, Ng4 required Bd4. After Ne5-c6 attacked Bd4, Bf2 opened Qa5-b4-c3 and allowed Qxc3. b3 had removed c3's pawn guard; Qd1 did not defend it. Saving the screen lost the target behind it.
- Rc1 attacked Qc3 but Bh6 reached c1 through empty g5/f4/e3/d2. Bxc1 Qxc1 cost R for B. A queen attack did not force retreat.
- Qh3 landed on Be6-f5-g4-h3: Bxh3 gxh3 lost Q for B. My pawn on f4 did NOT block f5. Inspect every remote bishop on a queen destination.
- Rxc4/bxc4 recovered a rook. gxh7+ discovered Rg1's check, but Qxg1+/Kxg1 still left Black R versus B. Bd3 guarded h7 while Kh8 blockaded it; the light bishop could not control h8.
- Bc4 tried to stop a3-a2 but landed undefended on Rf4's clear fourth rank. Ke3 did not guard c4; Rxc4 removed B. A blockading purpose does not make its destination safe.
- Finished 7:31, zero invalid. Bd4/Qh3/Bc4 took 50/37/32 seconds: explicit screen and bishop-ray scans were missing.

## T38/T37/T36 Taimanov lessons
- Be2/O-O/Be3/f4/Kh1/Bf3 gives playable central pressure. Qe1 was inaccurate in T36/T38; compare central play. After Qxe5 compare immediate queen exchange rather than automatic Nxc6.
- e5 hits Nf6 and clears Bf3's diagonal. Against d6, exf6 took N and supported Qxg7#. Wins depended on errors.
- Qg3/Kg8 with a clear g-file pins g7. Nxf6+ can remove B and fork Re8 without legal gxf6. Kf8 releases the pin but may abandon Re8 to Nxe8.
- Qd5 hit Qe6/Rd7 and had Nc3/Rd1 support, yet Rxd5 won Q for R: Black could STOP exchanging. Re7 also landed on Nd5's capture square. Check cheaper capturers before claiming pressure.

## Maroczy geometry
- Qa5 BEFORE f3 pins Nc3 to Ke1; Be3 leaves e4 without a legal guard. With f3/Qxd4, Ng4 hits Be3 AND uncovers Bg7 against Qd4.
- O-O-O/Rhe1: Nc3 screens Qa5-e1; Rd1 guards Re1. Nxd5 removes the screen; Bd3/Bxd3/Rxd3 abandons Re1. Central exchanges can clear the c-file around Kc1.
- Rfd1 allowed Bxc4 Bxc4 Rxc4, losing P. Nd5 clears Rc8-c1; guarding d1 does not stop dxc1=Q.
- Short castling: Nd5 clears Qa5-d2; queen exchange and Bxd5/exd5 enable Bd4+, forking Kg1/Ra1 with b2 empty. c5 allows Rxc5; Bc4 loses to b5xc4. Re1 ignored Rf7-f4 against Bf4.
