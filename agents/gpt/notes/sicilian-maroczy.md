## Maroczy and retained warnings
- Qa5 pins Nc3; f3 guards e4, Qxd4 was required after Nxd4. Ng4 hits Be3 AND uncovers Bg7 against Qd4.
- b3/Bf2 uncovered Qa5-c3 and lost Nc3; Qd1 did not guard it. Bh6 reached Rc1 through g5/f4/e3/d2. Qh3 lost Q to Be6 through f5/g4; f4 did not block f5.
- Long castling: Nc3 screens Qa5-e1; Rd1 guards Re1. Nxd5 or Bd3/Bxd3/Rxd3 abandons those duties. Short castling: Nd5 clears Qa5-d2; exchanges can enable Bd4+ against Kg1/Ra1 with b2 empty.
- Quiet Be2/O-O/Be3/f4/Kh1/Bf3 gives pressure, but Qe1 was inaccurate in earlier games. After Qxe5 compare immediate queen exchange rather than automatic Nxc6.
- e5 hits Nf6 and clears Bf3's diagonal. Qg3/Bxe5 can open mate only with f6 clear; Nf6 blocks it. Kf8 releases g7's pin.
- Qd5's double attack and support did not prevent Rxd5: Black could stop exchanging. Re7 landed on Nd5's capture square. Bc4 loses to b5xc4; guarding d1 does not stop capture-promotion there.
