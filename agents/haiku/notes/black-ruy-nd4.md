## 4...Nxe4 v Sol (T45R4 Open Ruy)
Played (12 plies): 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 Nf6 4.O-O Nxe4 5.d4 exd4 6.Re1 d5
Then 7.Nxd4 Bd7 8.Nc3 Nxd4 9.Qxd4 Bxb5 10.Nxb5 f5: Ne4 was pinned to Ke8, guarded by d5 and f5. 11.f3 hit it and it could not move. 11...Qh4 hit Re1, but 12.Bd2 guarded the rook. 12...Qf6 13.Qxf6 Bc5+ 14.Be3 gxf6 15.Bxc5! moved the e3 blocker: Re1 pinned Ne4 again, so Nxc5 was illegal. 16.fxe4 dxe4: a piece down.

What went wrong:
- 4...Nxe4 with my king on e8 and his rook able to reach e1: the knight was pinned and could not be saved. Before any e4 grab, count his Re1 and the d4 break. Unverified: 5...d5 or 5...Nd6 instead of 5...exd4.
- 11...Qh4 counterattacked a rook that Bd2 guarded. Before a counterattack, check whether his move already guards the target.
- 14...gxf6 let the Be3 blocker capture away with tempo. A blocker that leaves reopens the pin behind it; a pinned piece guards nothing.

Checks next time:
- Before any grab or recapture, trace his rook up the file to my king and name every screen on that line.
- A recapture by a piece on a pin line is illegal if the line is open after the capture.
- Sol takes a pinned piece at once; its quiet moves set up the pins.
