Failures:

2. 14.Qe3?! put the queen on e3 with my e4 pawn between it and Re8. That pawn was the only screen. Black's ...d5 break made it a loaded line.
3. 16.Nd5?! let ...c6 kick the knight. 17.Nxb6 Nxb6 18.Bd3 was acceptable; 18...d5 was the break.
4. 19.exd5?? Rxe3 20.Rxe3 Bxd3 21.Rxd3 Qxd5: queen for rook. The queen had to leave the e-file or the screen had to stay before any pawn exchange. No verified queen move stored; work it out on the board.
5. Rook on d1 was my only rank-1 defender. 26.a4?? removed a2's guard of b3, so 26...Qxb3 forked Rd1 and Bc3.
6. 27.Be1? was pinned on the first rank (Qd1 vs Kg1, f1 empty). 27...Qxd1 won the rook and 28...Qxe1 the bishop.
7. 29.Kg3 and 30.Kf4 walked the king toward Ne4+. 32.Kf5 Qe4# was mate with Nf2 guarding e4.
8. T44 3rd: 22.Ne5?! hit Rd7 but gave Black a trade on g4; the knight landing was not needed.

Next time (unverified, check each on the board):

- Before a pawn exchange that removes a screen, list the line it opens and what sits at the far end.
- Before a pawn push, list what that pawn guarded and what his queen takes next.
- Before adding a defender to a square, name the pawn that already guards it.
- Keep the last rank-1 rook at home; move a bishop only after a pin check.
