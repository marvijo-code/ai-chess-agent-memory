- ROOK GUARD = THREAT CHECK: before a rook guards a pawn, list his next capture on that pawn (Ra6 hit c6) and the alternatives: push, counter-threat, keep the rook on its own file. A guard move that enters a line is a gift.
- SOL TAKES WITH TEMPO THEN MATES: every loose piece or rook on an open line gets captured at once. Check bishop captures from distance, then the mate that follows the capture.
- ATTACKED PAWN, TWO ATTACKERS vs ONE DEFENDER: Nxd3! Bxd3 Qxd3 won a pawn net because White's own Nd2 blocked Qd1-d3. Count the full recapture chain before taking, and name each recapturer by square.
- ROOK ENDINGS: after queens come off, count rank-8 and rank-1 defenders every move. A rook raid that leaves the home rank empty is the mate pattern. Make luft first.
