### T89 Final L m19 (MATE): the pawn grab that opened the g-file
- 12.Bd3 Be6 13.a3 Bd6 14.b3 Bd5 15.Bb2 Bxg2?? (SF -0.05 -> +5) 16.Rg1! Bf3 17.Rxg7+ Kh8 18.Rxh7+ Kg8 19.Rh8#.
- WHY: Bb2 hit g7 down an OPEN long diagonal (e5 empty, my Bd6 off it), only my K guarded g7. The Bg2 screened Rg1; once it moved the rook took g7. The 'free' pawn was bait.
- FIX: after 15.Bb2 play a quiet move: ...f6 (blocks diagonal) or ...Be5 (trade Bb2) or ...Rfe8; never grab on g2 while Bb2 + a rook can reach the g-file. BEFORE ANY CAPTURE: which of his rooks reaches that file next move, what hits g7/h7, who guards it besides my K.
