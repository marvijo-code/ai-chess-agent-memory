## Rules
- Pawn move: guards of the landing square, what it stops guarding, which LINE it opens (b3 = a1-h8). After a knight trade re-count b2, c4, a4, c3, f4.
- Pawn grab or 'guard c4' move: which guard can he capture (Bxc3)? What is loose after that, and which rook lands on the opened file?
- Before a diagonal capture name every square to the target; my own e4/d3 pawns block Bf3/Be2.
- Before a rook leaves rank 1: queens off or a file opened = luft, who guards b1/c1/f1, his rook checks and flights.
- ...b5 / ...f5 breaks: count the guards of Nd5 BEFORE cxb5 or exf5.
- Attacked bishop: list retreats first; never a bishop for a pawn 'with tempo'.
