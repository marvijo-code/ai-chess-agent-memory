## Key lessons (read before EVERY move)
- KING-FILE SCAN (T22R1 17.dxc5? Qc7 18.b4? a5 19.a3?? axb4 20.axb4 Ra1#): Kb1 + b-pawn pushed + his Bf6 on the long diagonal = a1 covered and the a-file opens. Before ANY pawn move next to my king or any pawn grab list: his bishop rays to a1/b2/g2, the file after axb4 axb4, his rook entry squares. A pawn up never beats a thin king; keep c3/b2 closed.
- THREAT SCAN FIRST (T21R1 25.Qe4! dxe5?? 26.Qh7+ Kf8 27.Qh8#): after EVERY move of his list what each Q/B/R/N hits NOW: batteries (Q before Bb1/Bc2 on b1-h7), his checks BY SQUARE, my king's flights.
- OUTPOST SCAN (T21F 14...c5? 15.cxd5 Nxd5 16.Nc4! Qc6 17.Nd6+): before a pawn break or queen move list his knight jumps to d6/e6/f7 with tempo.
- UNMASK + LOOSE SWEEP (T21SF2G1 12...Nxe5!; T21R3 14...e6?? 15.Bxd4): find his pieces guarded only through a line I can open; with queens off list MY unguarded pieces every move.
- PAWN MOVES NEAR MY KING (T21R1 23...h6?): ...h6/...g6 empty h7/g7; answer a Q+B battery with ...g6/...Nf6.
- GRAB CHECK (T20R3 28...Nxd5?? Qd2 guarded d5): before ANY capture list every guard of the target incl. pawns and x-rays, MY recapture, and what the capturing piece screens (T17R1 36.Nxc2?? unmasked Qb6-f2; T19SF2G1G1 32.Nxc4?? bxc4).
- CLOCK: book 1-5 s; quiet 10-15 s; 45 s max at captures/breaks. Under 8:00 cap 15 s, under 4:00 cap 8, under 1:30 cap 3. Long thinks never fixed a bad plan (T21F 50 s x 12; T22R1 61 s on 19.a3?? missed mate in 1). Armageddon White: level is not lost; Black: a draw wins, build a fortress.
- HIS QUEEN WITH TEMPO (T20R2 25...Re8? 26.Qd3!): before a rook move/trade list EVERY queen move of his that hits my queen.
- ILLEGAL MOVES (3 = forfeit): trace paths square by square incl. my own pieces and his just-pushed pawn; list pins.
- HIS LAST MOVE FIRST (T18SF2G1G1 34...Bxd5?? 35.Bxb8): if it attacks my piece, save it; a free pawn is bait. Before a piece goes to the edge list every retreat and pawn kick (T18R3 18...Nb4? 19.a3). My own piece can block my line (T19R2 11.Bd3?? d4!).
- PLAN, NOT DRIFT: name a plan every move; list HIS breaks (after ...c5 and ...a5 what opens?); before ...b4/...a3/...f5 write my recapture. As White vs Chigorin AVOID d5.
- DOWN/CONVERSION: SF/Sol convert a piece up; DeepSeek hangs things, so make threats. Up: trade, no bait, every unit guarded, stalemate check every ply, vary at the FIRST repeat (T15R3, T20SF2G1 slips).
