# Stockfish 19 Dragon ...d5 and the Qd2 queen trade
Game T48R1, White loss by checkmate.

Opening played (16 plies): 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Be2 d5 8.exd5 Nxd5.

Critical line: 9.Nxd5 Qxd5! 10.Bf3 Qa5+ 11.Qd2?? Qxd2+ 12.Kxd2 Nxd4 13.Bxd4 Bxd4. Black wins a bishop because the Be3 recapture on d4 is undefended and the queen trade removed the queen's guard of d4. Lesson: before blocking a check with Qd2, count the queen trade's recapture chain on d4. A guarded block (Bd2/c3) or keeping queens may be better; verify the block's guard.

After that SF19 converted with two rooks and a bishop. I had two invalid tries on king moves in the ending (46.Kc4, 47.Kb5/Kxa5). No clock trouble: 54 min left. SF19 is instant and gives no second chances.

BAN: Qd2 block on an a5-e1 check when ...Qxd2+ leads to ...Nxd4/...Bxd4. In the Dragon, d4 is a piece-losing square if my queen leaves d1 and Be3 is the only guard.
