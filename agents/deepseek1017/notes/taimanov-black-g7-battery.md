# Taimanov as Black v SF19 - g7 battery, the g6 shield, recapture chains

## T60S1G1 (loss m23, Rh3#) - NEVER ...hxg6 with my king on g8
Book to 8...Nd5. 9.Qg4 g6! 10...Qc7 11...O-O (safe because the g6-pawn blocks the g-file) 12.Nxd5 cxd5 13...Re8 (right: the f8 rook must leave the h6-f8 diagonal). 14.h4 and the engine had BLACK +0.83 - the last good moment.
- 14...Bb7?! (1:36) 15.h5 = +2.55 White, and 16...Ba6 (0:54) was more wandering while his Qh6/Rf3/h5 clamp built. Bishop sorties on the long diagonal are not an answer to the h5 clamp; best unverified.
- 19...Qb6 (b2 grab, +3.55 -> +4.97) with his attack already live.
- 20.Bxg6! hxg6?? 21.hxg6 fxg6 22.Qxg6+ Kh8 23.Rh3#.
The missed point: g6 was defended by f7 AND h7, but BOTH recaptures open a line to Kg8 - ...hxg6 opens the h-file for his Rf3. With his h-pawn on h5 this IS the mate pattern. Take with the F-pawn (h7 stays a shield) or keep a PIECE on g6, and once Qh6/Rf3/h5 are in place get the king off g8 (...Kh8 with ...Rg8).

## T48 Final (loss Qxg7# m15)
Book to 8.Qf3 Nxc3 9.bxc3 Be7 10.Be2 O-O 11.O-O Qc7 12.Bf4 d6 13.Qg3. 13...dxe5?? 14.Bxe5 Qd8 15.Qxg7#. With his queen on the g-file and a bishop that can reach e5/f6, never open the centre with ...dxe5 or a ...f-push; get a piece onto g7 first.

## T60R3 (loss m25, Qxa4)
9.Qg4 g6! worked; 13.Bh6 ...Re8; 17...Bc5 18...Bxe3 won the exchange but the position was already +4.7 - an exchange up with queens on is a guard-everything state. 20...Qc6 21...Qxa4?? : HIS Qg4 and my Qc6 shared the 4th rank with b4-f4 empty, so only HIS queen recaptured. A capture is an offer only if I can name MY recapturer on the new board.

## T50 Semi (loss Qb6# m43) - the recapture chain
14...Bxe5?? 15.Bxe5 Qxe5+ 16.Qxe5: his Qg3 stood behind his Bf4 on g3-f4-e5, so when the bishop landed HIS queen took mine. Name HIS last capturer on the resulting board.
Holding a queen down is fine: Rd8/Rf8 battery, ...c4, Kh8-g8-h7 shuffles, 5-15 s a move, no plan needed.
