# White Dragon Yugoslav v Stockfish 19 - Ng4/Bc8 trap, f4? and the a7 grab (T49R2, T51R2, T52 Final)

Opening played, 16 plies: 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Bc4 d6 8.Bb3 Ng4.

SF's 8...Ng4 is a bishop-backed outpost: the c8-bishop guards g4 through d7-e6-f5, so the knight is DEFENDED. NEVER 9.Qxg4?? Bxg4 (T49R2: queen for a knight, mate m28). Standard answer: 9.Nxc6! bxc6 10.Bg5. T52 Final used it and SF marked it !; the loss came later.

## T49R2 - Qxg4??
I called Ng4 'completely undefended' after 5 s and lost my queen to Bxg4. Correct: 9.Nxc6 bxc6 10.Bg5, or h3 only after counting ...Nxe3. A knight landing on g4 is defended by the bishop that fianchettoed path d7-e6-f5.

## T51R2 - 9.h3?! Nxe3! and 15.Nf6+?? exf6
9.h3?! met 9...Nxe3! taking my Be3; 10.fxe3 doubled my e-pawns and 14...Bxb2 won a pawn. Prefer 9.Nxc6. Later 15.Nf6+?? failed to 15...exf6: f6 is covered by BOTH his e7 and g7 PAWNS, so a check can be taken by a pawn. Before any knight check/fork, list every enemy PAWN capture square of the landing square.

## T52 Final Arm - 16.f4?/18.Bxa7? and ...Rxe4+!
Opening to move 8 as above. Then 9.Nxc6! bxc6 10.Bg5 Qa5 11.Qd2 Rb8 12.Rb1?! h6 13.Bf4 g5 14.Bg3 Ba6 15.h3 Nf6 16.f4? (16 plies) - and now 16...Nh5 17.Bf2 Nxf4 won the f4 pawn. The f4 push loosened e4/f4 while his knight could reach h5; with my king still on e1 I needed to castle/answer the knight, not push f4.

Then 18.Bxa7? grabbed a7 while his Nf4 was loose in my camp: 18...Rb4 19.a3 Bxc3! 20.Qxc3 Rxe4+! 21.Be3 Rxe3+ 22.Kd1 Rxc3, and my queen fell. The b8-rook became a monster on the b-file; a7 was poison, not a pawn.

Rules from this:
- No f4 in the Dragon while his f6-knight can go h5 and take it; f4 needs king safety and a way to meet ...Nh5.
- No pawn grab on a7/a2 while his knight is on f4/h5 in my camp and my king is uncastled; answer the knight first.
- Rook check in my camp: block on a DIFFERENT rank from my queen. 21.Be3? stood on e3, my queen on c3, same rank; ...Rxe3+ then ...Rxc3 took both.

## Still useful
After ...d5 in a Dragon, do NOT block his Qa5+ with Qd2??; use a guarded block (Bd2/c3) and count the queen-trade recapture chain on d4. See notes/stockfish-dragon-queen-trade.md.
