# White Dragon Yugoslav v Stockfish 19 - the Ng4/Bc8 trap, h3 and the f6 pawn (T49R2, T51R2 both lost)

Opening played (16 plies): 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 g6 5.Nc3 Bg7 6.Be3 Nf6 7.Bc4 d6 8.Bb3 Ng4.

SF's 8...Ng4 attacks nothing I own; it is a bishop-backed outpost. The c8-bishop guards g4 through d7-e6-f5, so the knight is DEFENDED TWICE (knight came from f6, guard Bc8).

## T49R2: 10.Qxg4?? Bxg4
I took the knight calling it 'completely undefended' (5 s think). 10...Bxg4 won my QUEEN for a knight, mate on move 28. Correct answers to 8...Ng4, in order of confidence:
- 9.Nxc6 bxc6 10.h3 Nf6 (or ...Ne5/Bh6) 11.f3/O-O - bishop pair and centre, no queen exposure.
- h3 only after the ...Nxe3 count below.
- Bg5 with tempo on f6/g4 only when the bishop is safe on g5 (his h6/e5 pawns).

## T51R2: 9.h3?! Nxe3! and 15.Nf6+?? exf6
9.h3?! (marked inaccuracy) met 9...Nxe3! : the knight took my Be3 and 10.fxe3 recaptured with the f-pawn. Black gets bishop for knight, my e-pawns are doubled, and 14...Bxb2 won the b2 pawn - SF was already +1. A kick is not a refutation; count ...Nxe3 first and prefer 9.Nxc6 bxc6 10.Bg5.

After 13.Nf3?! Nxf3+ 14.Qxf3 Bxb2 I was two pawns down and tried 15.Nf6+?? - a 'fork of king and rook'. 15...exf6! : f6 is covered by BOTH his e7 and g7 PAWNS, so the check was taken by a pawn and I lost a whole knight for nothing (+7 for SF). RULE: before any knight check or fork, list every enemy PAWN capture square of the landing square; a check that lands on a pawn's capture square is a gift, not a fork.

Down a piece vs SF the rest was a clean conversion: ...d5, ...Rxe3, ...Rg3+/...Rg4+ drove my king to h1, Qxd5+ 37.Rf3 Qxf3#. Same class as other losses: down material v SF there is no counterplay, only guarding.

Further lesson from T49R2: down a queen for a knight vs SF every 'attack' (h5-h6, f4, exd6) let his queen checks grow (Qxb2+, Qa3, Qc5+, Qe3+, Qxe2#). After such a loss: guard every unit, trade nothing into his queen's files, expect mate in 10-15 moves.
