# Taimanov as Black - guards: c6, g7, b5, e5 retreat, and the queen's landing square

Opening pattern (Taimanov, 14 plies): 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 e6 4.d4 cxd4 5.Nxd4 Nf6 6.Nxc6 bxc6 7.e5 Nd5, then 8.Qf3 with the queen aiming at f6/g3 (T49 Final, T50R1). T51S2G1 met 8.Be3 then 9.f4 Qc7 10.Qd2 Nxd4 11.Bxd4 b5.

## The c6 guard (T49 Final)
After ...bxc6 the c6 pawn has NO recapturer: b7 is empty, Bc8 and Qd8 do not reach c6. So 11...O-O? met 12.Bxc6!, winning the a8 rook. Order: ...Qc7 FIRST (it guards c6 and meets Bxc6 with ...Qxc6), then castle. Also 8...Nxc3 9.bxc3 gives White c3+d4+e5; take that trade only for a concrete gain.

## The g7 guard with an uncastled king (T50R1)
8...Qc7 9.Qg3 h6 10.Ne4: with my king on e8 only Bf8 guards g7. 10...Be7? stepped it off; 11.Qxg7 Bf8 12.Qxh8 won a rook. ...h6 is luft only for a king on g8. At move 10: ...O-O, or keep Bf8 home and develop ...d6/...Bd7 once g7 is covered. Before any ...Be7/...Bd6 step with his queen near g3 write 'what guards g7 after this?'.

## Down a whole rook
12...Nb4?? (after 5:28) 'forked' c2 but 13.Nf6+ won the f8 bishop with check and 14.Qxf8# mated. Down material v SF: guard every unit, 5-10 s moves, audit every enemy knight landing for check + fork + mate. No counterplay grab. 0 illegal, 54:54 left.

## b5 needs a recapturer, and no queen grab (T51R3, loss m29)
Taimanov with his 9.f4: 9...Nxd4 10.Bxd4 b5?! 11.e5! Ne8 12.Nxb5! - his Nc3 took b5 and NOTHING recaptured: my a6-pawn was still home, and ...Bb7/...Qc7 do not guard b5. Rule: play ...a6 BEFORE ...b5 while his Nc3 can land on b5; a pawn push whose landing square has no named recapturer is a gift.
Then 12...Qxc2?? 'restoring material': his Qd1 covers c2, 13.Qxc2! won my queen, and after that a queen up he simply took everything (Nc7 fork, Bxa7, Qxe8+, Qd8+/Qf6#). A queen sortie is never the answer to a lost pawn - list the attackers of her landing square from the board, his QUEEN first.

## The e5 retreat: the f6-knight must not go to e8 (T51R3, T51S2G1)
When his e5 hits Nf6, ...Ne8 is a trap twice over: T51R3 11...Ne8? 14.Nc7 forked my a8-rook and e8-knight; T51S2G1 12...Ne8 18.Nxd7 won my d7 pawn, hit the f8-rook, and 19.Nb6 then hit a8-rook + c8-bishop (18...Rf7?? did nothing for the a8/c8 fork). With my a8-rook and c8-bishop still home, send the knight to d5 (take Nxd5 exd5, count the guards) or h5 - not to e8.

## ...Qxc2 and the ...Bb7/...Qxg2# net (T51S2G1, win m27)
12.e5 f6 13.exf6 Bxf6 14.Ne4 Bxd4+ 15.Qxd4 Qxc2: c2 was free ONLY because his Qd1 had gone to d4 (contrast T51R3 where Qd1 still guarded c2). Then 17...Qxe2 won the Be2 for good. With ...b5/...Bb7, his Kg1, his f2-rook gone and g2 held only by the king, 27...Qxg2# is mate - the b7 bishop protects the queen. Watch for that net every time his f2-rook leaves or his queen steps off g2's defence.
