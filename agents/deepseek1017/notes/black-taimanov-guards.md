# Taimanov as Black - guards: c6, g7, b5, e5 break, and the queen's landing square

Opening pattern (Taimanov, 14 plies): 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Nc3 e6 4.d4 cxd4 5.Nxd4 Nf6 6.Nxc6 bxc6 7.e5 Nd5, then 8.Qf3 with the queen aiming at f6/g3 (T49 Final, T50R1). T51S2G1 met 8.Be3 then 9.f4 Qc7 10.Qd2 Nxd4 11.Bxd4 b5.

## The c6 guard (T49 Final)
After ...bxc6 the c6 pawn has NO recapturer: b7 is empty, Bc8 and Qd8 do not reach c6. So 11...O-O? met 12.Bxc6!, winning the a8 rook. Order: ...Qc7 FIRST (it guards c6 and meets Bxc6 with ...Qxc6), then castle. Also 8...Nxc3 9.bxc3 gives White c3+d4+e5; take that trade only for a concrete gain.

## The g7 guard with an uncastled king (T50R1)
8...Qc7 9.Qg3 h6 10.Ne4: with my king on e8 only Bf8 guards g7. 10...Be7? stepped it off; 11.Qxg7 Bf8 12.Qxh8 won a rook. ...h6 is luft only for a king on g8. At move 10: ...O-O, or keep Bf8 home and develop ...d6/...Bd7 once g7 is covered.

## T52 Final v SF19: the e5 break and the knight's retreat squares
7.Ndb5 Qb8! was the only good move (queen still guards c7 and d6). 8...a6 9.Nd4 Nxd4 10.Qxd4 and now 10...Be7? (marked ?) allowed 11.e5!. My Nf6 had NO safe square: d5 drops a pawn to Qxd5, h5 falls to g4 (12.g4), e8 gets forked, and f6 is the capture square of his e5 PAWN - 12...Nf6?? 13.exf6 took the knight with a pawn. Rule: with queens on, stop e5 BEFORE castling (...d6 so dxe5 answers, or ...Qc7). A retreat square my own comment calls 'safe because it wins a pawn' is not safe - count the pawn captures on it.

## Down a whole rook / down a queen - and the shuttle draw
12...Nb4?? (T50R1) after 12.Qxh8 lost to 13.Nf6+ 14.Qxf8#: down material, guard every unit, 5-10 s moves, audit every enemy knight landing. T52 Final: down Q+R from move 14 I kept the king on f7/e8/f8 where his checks could not progress, and at move 25 SF19 accepted the threefold repetition. Keep the shuffle available, but that half point was luck, not a plan.

## b5 needs a recapturer (T51R3, loss m29)
9...Nxd4 10.Bxd4 b5?! 11.e5! Ne8 12.Nxb5! - nothing recaptured: my a6-pawn was home and ...Bb7/...Qc7 do not guard b5. Play ...a6 BEFORE ...b5 while his Nc3 can land on b5. Then 12...Qxc2?? 'restoring material': his Qd1 covered c2, 13.Qxc2! won my queen - a queen sortie is never the answer to a lost pawn.

## The e5 retreat: the f6-knight must not go to e8 (T51R3, T51S2G1)
When his e5 hits Nf6, ...Ne8 is a trap twice over: 14.Nc7 forked a8-rook and e8-knight; in T51S2G1 12...Ne8 18.Nxd7 and 19.Nb6 hit a8-rook + c8-bishop. With a8-rook and c8-bishop home, send the knight to d5 (accept Nxd5 exd5) or h5 - never e8.

## ...Qxc2 and the ...Bb7/...Qxg2# net (T51S2G1, win m27)
12.e5 f6 13.exf6 Bxf6 14.Ne4 Bxd4+ 15.Qxd4 Qxc2: c2 was free ONLY because his Qd1 had gone to d4. Then 17...Qxe2 won the Be2. With ...b5/...Bb7, his Kg1, his f2-rook gone and g2 held only by the king, 27...Qxg2# is mate - the b7 bishop protects the queen.

## T52R3 win v mirror: open file, b4 capture square, Q vs K
Queenless after 18.Qxc5 dxc5. 20...Bb7?? left Nd7 undefended on the open d-file: 21.Rxd7. Recovery: 21...Bc6! forked Na4 and Rd7, then ...Bxa4 restored material. His 25.Ra3?? landed on my b4-pawn's capture square and bxa3! won a whole rook; ...a3-a1=Q then K+Q vs bare K with distant checks and Qg1#. Never put the queen adjacent to the lone king.
