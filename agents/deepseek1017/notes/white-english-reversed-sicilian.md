# White English v 1...e5 (reversed Sicilian) - the squeeze setup

Setup (9 plies): 1.c4 e5 2.Nc3 Nf6 3.g3 d5 4.cxd5 Nxd5 5.Bg2, then Nf3/O-O/d4 and the bishop pair on the long diagonal.

## T58 Armageddon v SF19, 0-1 m30 - the two bad destinations
Clamp line, 16 plies: 1.c4 Nf6 2.Nc3 e6 3.g3 d5 4.cxd5 exd5 5.Nf3 d4 6.Nb1 Be7 7.e3 dxe3 8.fxe3 Na6, then 9.Bg2 O-O 10.O-O Be6 11.d3 Nb4.
- The build held but SF edged up (+0.4 after 6.Nb1, +1 after 7.e3 dxe3 8.fxe3): treat the clamp as a 20-move defensive project, 10-20 s a move, every unit named.
- 11...Nb4 outpost: its jumps are d3/c2/a2/a6/c6/d5. Answer with pieces (Qe1), never a pawn kick; d3 must stay guarded twice.
- 12.Ne5/13.Nc3/14.Nc4 built an active knight; fine. Then 15.Be4?? Nxe4! - e4 is the f6-knight's jump square and NOTHING else guarded the bishop. After 16.Nxe4 Nxd3! SF was +6; then 17.Ncd2 18.Qc2 19.Rf3 and 19...Ne1! 20.Qd1 Nxf3+ 21.Qxf3 lost the exchange.
- 23.Qg6?? hxg6 finished it: g6 is the h7-pawn's capture square. Queen for a pawn while already behind.
Lesson: in this line (and every line) the destination sweep is the whole game - list the enemy knight jumps onto any bishop/knight/-rook square and the pawn that hits any queen square before the move goes in.

## T57R1 v GLM, 1-0 checkmate
- Same symmetric branch as T55R5.3: ...Nxc3, bxc3, ...Bd6, ...O-O, ...Nc6, d4 exd4 cxd4, ...Bg4, Be3, ...Bxf3 Bxf3. Then his ...Nxd4?? fell to Bxd4 and his ...Bc5?? fell to Bxc5; both were undefended pieces, and the queen trade that followed left me B+B+5P v R+5P.
- In that ending keep the bishops mutually supporting and use one to cover the pawn his rook eyes: 20.Bd5 guarded a2, so 21...Rxa2?? 22.Bxa2 won his rook.
- Conversion: e-pawn advanced to e6, king walked Kf3-e4-d5-c6-c7, Bxa3 stopped his last passer, then g4/g5 plus f4/f5 made a wedge; f6+ was guarded by g5, f7 covered by a bishop, f8=Q+ was guarded, and Qh6# came with g5 covering h6 and Be6 covering g8.
- Rule: when a piece lands undefended on one of my bishop's lines, take it; GLM hands knights, bishops and rooks this way. Keep bishops off his pawns' capture squares and off his rook lines.

## T55R5.3 v GLM, win on time
- Symmetric ...Nxc3/...Bd6/...Nc6/...exd4. After cxd4 and ...Bg4 11.Be3 Bxf3 12.Bxf3 I had the two bishops and a broad centre; 13.Qd2 / 14.Rad1 were level. 15.d5?! kicked his Nc6 to e5, hitting Bf3; cleaner Qd2/Re1 building first, d5 later.
- 18.Bg5 Qxg5?? 19.Qxg5 = +8; one move later 20.Qxh6?? gxh6 gave her back for a pawn. h6 is the g7-pawn's capture square.
- Recovery: loose pawns with the rook, 29.cxd7! pawn-takes-rook, 30.d8=Q safe, then 31.Qxd6.
- One illegal try: 15.e4 with my own Be3 still on e3.
