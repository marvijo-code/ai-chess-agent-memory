Setup (9 plies): 1.c4 e5 2.Nc3 Nf6 3.g3 d5 4.cxd5 Nxd5 5.Bg2, then Nf3/O-O/d4 and the bishop pair on the long diagonal.

## T59R2 v GLM 5.3 Flash, 1-0 on time - won queen, gave her back, won anyway
The symmetric branch once more: ...Nxc3 6.bxc3, ...Bd6, O-O, ...Nc6, d4 exd4 cxd4, ...Be6, Be3, ...Qe7 (one illegal Qc7 try - own pawn), Qd2, ...Rad8.
- 13.Bg5 hit the e7-queen. It answered 13...h6?? - a 'kick' that fails because his queen stood on e7 in my bishop's f6-e7 line: 14.Bxe7 Nxe7 won her outright (+6). Rule: HIS pawn kick is only legal cover when the attacked piece can step to a square NOT on my bishop's line; check his whole build for a loose queen before believing a kick.
- Then 15.Ne5 Bxe5 16.dxe5?? - MY OWN BLUNDER: the d4-pawn left the d-file and his Rd8xd2 took my queen, +Q down to level. Before any pawn capture/push name the file and diagonals the vacated square opens onto my queen/king.
- Recovered with 17.Rfe1, 18.Bxb7, then the rooks: 21.Rexd1 (his Rd1) left me 2R+B v R+B+N; 23.Bxf5 Bxf5 exchanged his last bishop, 24.Rac1/25.Rc6/26.Ra6/27.Rd7 squeezed the frozen c7 and a7 pawns (two attackers against one defender), 28.Rxc7, 29.Raxa7 Rxa7 30.Rxa7 won his rook, 31.e6/32.e7 and his clock went 0:24 v my ~1:00:00.
- Legality note: 29.Raxa7 needed the file letter; plain Rxa7 was rejected once (two rooks on the a-file).

## T58 Armageddon v SF19, 0-1 m30 - the two bad destinations
Clamp line, 16 plies: 1.c4 Nf6 2.Nc3 e6 3.g3 d5 4.cxd5 exd5 5.Nf3 d4 6.Nb1 Be7 7.e3 dxe3 8.fxe3 Na6, then 9.Bg2 O-O 10.O-O Be6 11.d3 Nb4. SF edged up; treat the clamp as a 20-move defensive project, 10-20 s a move.
- 11...Nb4 outpost: answer with pieces (Qe1), never a pawn kick; d3 guarded twice.
- 15.Be4?? Nxe4! - e4 is the f6-knight's jump square and nothing else guarded the bishop. Then 19...Ne1! 20.Qd1 Nxf3+ lost the exchange; 23.Qg6?? hxg6 (h7-pawn's square) finished it.

## T57R1 v GLM, 1-0 checkmate
- Same symmetric ...Nxc3/...Bd6/...Nc6/...exd4 branch. His ...Nxd4?? fell to Bxd4 and his ...Bc5?? fell to Bxc5; both undefended, and the queen trade left me B+B+5P v R+5P. Keep bishops mutually supporting and one covering the pawn his rook eyes: 20.Bd5 guarded a2, so 21...Rxa2?? 22.Bxa2 won his rook. Conversion: e-pawn to e6, king walk Kf3-e4-d5-c6-c7, g4/g5 + f4/f5 wedge, Qh6#.

- After cxd4 and ...Bg4 11.Be3 Bxf3 12.Bxf3 I had the two bishops and a broad centre. 15.d5?! kicked his Nc6 to e5, hitting Bf3; cleaner Qd2/Re1 first, d5 later. 18.Bg5 Qxg5?? 19.Qxg5 = +8; 20.Qxh6?? gxh6 gave her back (g7-pawn's square). Recovery: 29.cxd7! pawn-takes-rook, 30.d8=Q safe.
