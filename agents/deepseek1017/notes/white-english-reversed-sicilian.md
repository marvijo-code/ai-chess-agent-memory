# White English v 1...e5 (reversed Sicilian) and the mirror reversed-QGD

## T60R1 v the mirror, DRAW (threefold) - a winning game thrown twice, then saved by a fortress
Reversed QGD build, 15 plies: 1.c4 Nf6 2.Nc3 e6 3.g3 d5 4.cxd5 exd5 5.Nf3 Be7 6.Bg2 O-O 7.O-O c6 8.d3, then ...Bf5 9.Nd4 ...Bg6 10.Be3 ...Nbd7 11.Qd2 ...c5 12.Nb3 ...Qc7 13.Nxd5! Nxd5 14.Bxd5 = pawn up + bishop pair (...Nb6 15.Bf3 ...Qd7 16.d4 ...c4 17.Nc5 ...Qc8 18.Ne4 Bxe4 19.Bxe4).
- BLUNDER 20.Bd3?? cxd3! His c4-pawn owns b3 AND d3. A pawn on MY half of the board is still a capturer; I checked his pieces on d3 and forgot the pawn. Bishop for a pawn from +1.
- Then ...Nd5/...Bf6/...Nxf4/...Qxd4 liquidated, and 27.Qxe5! Qxe5 28.fxe5 Bxe5 pulled the material back level (Q+2R each).
- BLUNDER 32.Rc6?? bxc6. A rook on the 6th/7th in his camp must have his a/b/c-pawn captures named first; the pawn needs no defender to take.
- The draw came from the fortress, not from tricks: Rc4 blockading his c5-pawn, king on e3/f3 beside e4, 42.f5! taking e6/g6 away from his rooks (a rook landing there falls to the pawn), no rook trade ever (R vs 2R), a4 freezing his a-pawn, h3/h4/h5 luft. He shuffled ...Rc7/...Rc6/...Kd6/...Ke5 for 25 moves and it ended threefold. Lesson: down a rook with pawns on the board, freeze his pawns and take the repetition instead of resigning.

## T59R3 v the mirror, 1-0 by mate - e5 fork and the rook-rank queen grab
Symmetric branch: ...Nxc3 6.bxc3, ...Bd6, O-O, ...Nbd7, d4, Be3, Qd2, Rad1, ...Qc7.
- 12.Nd4 hit the f5-bishop, 13.Bf4 offered the dark-bishop trade; he took on e5 and 15.Nf3 hit the queen with tempo. She went to d6.
- 16.Qe3 (defends e4), 17.Nh4 (kick the g6-bishop), 18.Qd2 (e4 free: Bg2 and Nc3 both guard it), then 19.e4! 20.f4! (f4 guards e5) 21.e5! - a fork of Qd6 and Nf6 where the e5-pawn is covered by f4, so Qxe5?? fxe5.
- 22...Qxd3?? landed his queen on my Rd1's rank: 23.Rxd3 Bxd3 24.Qxd3 = queen+bishop for a rook. Look for this whenever his queen takes a 'free' pawn on a line my rook controls.
- Conversion: keep my queen off the knight squares c5/c4/d5, trade the last rook (30.Rxd8+ Rxd8), then loose pawns (Qxc5, Qxd7, Bxb7, Qxe8+), 38.Qf8#.
