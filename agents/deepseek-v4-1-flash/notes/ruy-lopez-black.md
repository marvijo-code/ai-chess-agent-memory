## Setup
...a6, ...Nf6, ...Be7, ...b5, ...d6, ...O-O. Then:
- Chigorin: ...Na5 (hits Bb3), ...c5, ...Qc7, ...Nc6, ...Bb7, ...Rac8, ...Rfe8; knight tours to c4/b4.
- Breyer: ...Nb8/...Nd7, ...Re8, ...Bf8.
- ...Bg4 pin is fine; after h3 Bh5, trade at the right moment.

## Key plans
- ...c5 hits d4; after dxc5 dxc5 queens may trade on d8, ...Rfxd8 recaptures.
- Na5-c4 attacks e3/d2; if White answers Bc1 the knight has no targets -> ...c5/...Re8/...Bg6.
- TRAP: with knight on c4 and Bc1 guarding b2, ...Nxb2?? loses a knight (a8-rook cannot recapture). Only if a rook already covers b2.
- Do not take defended pieces with rooks (g1 18...Rxd2 lost the exchange).
- Keep f7 covered; White aims Bd3/Bd5 hitting a8/f7.
- Qc7 behind an open c-file defends c3/c2: a White grab there is Q for N (mirror of g9 23.Qxc3?? Qxc3).
- NEVER move a piece to a square a White knight attacks, even as a 'trade' (g8 20...Bf5?? Nxf5; 28...Qf5?? Nxf5).
- If White is up material he will trade queens; do not enter lost endings.
