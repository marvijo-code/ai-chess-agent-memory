## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- EVERY PAWN'S TWO FORWARD DIAGONALS = CAPTURE SQUARES, LISTED EVERY MOVE (T40R1 13.Bg5?? died to an h6-pawn pushed 3 MOVES earlier - the list runs against the CURRENT board, old pushes stay armed; T39R5: Bd6?? died to his e5-pawn, then his d6-passer ate my Qc7; T39R4 23...Rd4?? = R): before EVERY move list BOTH colours' pawn capture squares - BLACK: d6 c5/e5; e5 d4/f4; e4 d3/f3; f7 e6/g6; c5 b4/d4; b5 a4/c4; g7 h6/f6; h6 g5. WHITE: e5 d6/f6; d6 c7/e7; d4 c5/e5; e4 d5/f5; c4 b5/d5; f4 e5/g5. Never park or leave a piece there; an advanced pawn's capture can come one move LATER. A pawn also guards whatever stands on its diagonals: 'his Bf4 is loose' is FALSE if a pawn covers it. An enemy passer inside my camp is a tower - its two diagonals are no-go.
