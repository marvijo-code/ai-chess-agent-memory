## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- OCCUPANT OF THE CAPTURE SQUARE, NAMED FROM THE BOARD (T38R4 13.Qxd7??): before EVERY capture state what piece of HIS actually stands on the destination - colour AND type - plus all its guards. I 'traded queens' with his PAWN while his queen sat two squares away: Bxd7 = Q for P and the game. A plan does not move his pieces; read the destination square off the board first.
- MATERIAL = PIECE LIST, NOT NARRATIVE: after every capture write both armies out piece by piece (W: Q RR BB NN Px / B: ...). T38R4 I produced four different counts in six moves, all narrative, starting with 'won a piece' on an even trade. Same failure T37R3: 25 moves of swindling a lost endgame. Recount BEFORE choosing any plan.
- PLANNED RECAPTURE MUST BE LEGAL - PIN CHECK (T38R3 21...Kf8??): when my answer to his check/capture is '...pawn takes', FIRST trace the slider line BEHIND the pawn. His Qg3 on the g-file pinned g7: gxf6 did not exist. Same family as T37R7's exd5 fantasy. If the recapture is pinned, recalculate the ENTIRE reply before moving.
- HIS LAST MOVE'S FULL RAYS BEFORE MY PLAN (T38R2 24...Qa6): walk EVERY line from his new square against EVERY piece of mine. First question: 'what does his move attack - including MY queen?'. WORKED in T38R3: 16...Bxc6!, 18...Qd7! (his Bf3's f3-c6 diagonal, open once e4 cleared).
- ATTACKED PIECE -> COUNT ATTACKERS ON THE DESTINATION (T38R2 15.Nxe5??): guarded is not safe - his attackers v my defenders (2 v 1 died).
- PAWN GEOMETRY IN CAPTURE CHAINS: before any plan ending in '...then my pawn takes', name the capturing pawn's TWO forward diagonals on the CURRENT board: d6 hits c5/e5; e5 hits d4/f4 (black) AND d6/f6 (white); f7 hits e6/g6; c5 hits b4/d4; b5 hits a4/c4. Must run while lost and merely reacting.
- DESTINATION SWEEP ON EVERY MOVE, quiet and developing included: (a) adjacent enemy pawns' two capture squares, (b) enemy knights' 8 squares, (c) enemy sliders' lines walked square by square, (d) king flights. Recite FROM THE BOARD - a sweep recalled from memory is not a sweep.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY: c6 hits a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 (never d5); f6 hits d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; d7 hits b6/b8/c5/e5/f6/f8. 'Defender left' != 'undefended': after ANY exchange recount ALL CURRENT attackers.
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN (15...Qe7??): after HIS capture name each of MY pieces hitting the destination, line traced; play the even recapture rather than a counterattack or retreat.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS; ATTACKING MOVES ABANDON GUARDS: after any capture sweep the capturer's lines from its new square; and a move that attacks his piece still gives up whatever it guarded (T38R4 16.Re1?? left f2 bare -> Qxf2#). Attacking his queen by moving my own guard is NOT defense.
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE: count HIS defenders first; the attack must win on recapture arithmetic. Four recurrences.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES: never park a piece on an enemy pawn's forward diagonals (13...Bd7??).
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL: a bend = no guard.
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): a push removing a guard leaves the hole until replaced. Every move: which of my pieces/pawns have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE through my own pawns; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION + BLOCKED DEFENDER: the mover never defends its own destination; a guard line with any piece on it DOES NOT EXIST.
- QUEEN SALLY NEEDS MY RECAPTURER; FORK REALITY: my check/fork is real only if nothing captures my piece on its destination; before ANY queen move name the piece that recaptures for me.
- ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: a piece whose only guard is my queen is lost when his second attacker lands.
