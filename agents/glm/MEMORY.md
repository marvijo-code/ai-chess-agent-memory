## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- PAWN GEOMETRY IN CAPTURE CHAINS (killed T37R7): before ANY plan ending in '...then my pawn takes', name the capturing pawn's TWO FORWARD diagonals on the CURRENT board. Black pawns capture DOWNWARD: d6 hits c5/e5; e5 hits d4/f4 (never d5 or d6); b5 hits a4/c4 (never c6). If the destination is not one of the two, the plan does not exist. Recurrences: T36R1 axb6 illegal, T37R3 c6-passer hit d7, T37R7 exd5 fantasy that turned his Qd5?? gift back into a lost game.
- HIS PAWN ATTACK NEEDS NO HOLE (T37R7 12.e5!): a pawn capture ignores guards entirely - g7 AND Be7 'guarded' my f6-knight and exf6 still won N for P. When his push hits my piece, ask can it CAPTURE back or FLEE - never 'contain'. Real answer here: Nc6xe5 (the OTHER knight recaptures) or move Nf6 with a plan BEFORE e5 lands.
- DESTINATION SWEEP ON EVERY MOVE, quiet and developing included: for the destination square list (a) every adjacent enemy PAWN's two capture squares, (b) every enemy KNIGHT reciting its 8 squares, (c) every enemy slider's line walked square by square, (d) king flights for checks. T37R6 lost on (a) and (b): 12.Be3?? onto d4's capture square; 8.Nxe5?? under c6; 17.Qxc5?? under Nd7. Recite FROM THE BOARD - a sweep recalled from memory of the rule is not a sweep.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY, recited: c6 hits a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 (never d5); f6 hits d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; d7 hits b6/b8/c5/e5/f6/f8. 'Defender left' != 'undefended': after ANY exchange recount ALL CURRENT attackers of the square (8.Nxe5?? - the d5-knight left, the c6-knight stayed).
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN (17.Nd2?): if his capture can be met by an even recapture I verified, play THE RECAPTURE - a counterattack like Nd2 loses the second piece too.
- MY RECAPTURER BEFORE ANY RETREAT (15...Qe7??): after HIS capture name each of MY pieces hitting the destination, line traced. Retreating instead of an even recapture turns a trade into a gift.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS: after ANY enemy capture sweep the capturer's lines FROM ITS NEW SQUARE before moving anything near it.
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE (14...Bc5??, 19...Bg5??): count HIS defenders first; an attack must win on recapture arithmetic, not 'hit'. Four recurrences now - this is my worst single habit.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES (13...Bd7??): never park a piece on an enemy pawn's forward diagonals; handle the passer first.
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL (33.Bd6??): name the full unbroken line; a bend = no guard. HIS bishop attacks MY pawn pushes (12.b4).
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): a push that removes a guard leaves the hole until something replaces it. Every move: which of my pieces/pawns have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE (20.Rd8??): before any rook move to an open/semi-open file walk the defending line THROUGH MY OWN PAWNS. Never onto an enemy rook's file; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION + BLOCKED DEFENDER: the mover never defends its own destination; a guard line with any piece on it DOES NOT EXIST - walk it naming occupants.
- SHUFFLE = GUARD DUTY LOSS: before ANY move, name what the moved piece WAS guarding on the old board.
- QUEEN SALLY NEEDS MY RECAPTURER (22.Qxc6 = Q+B for Q+P): before ANY queen move name the piece that recaptures for me.
- FORK REALITY: my check/fork is real only if nothing captures my piece on its destination (8.Nc7+?? Qxc7).
- ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: a piece whose only guard is my queen is lost when his second attacker lands (13.g5?? Bxd4).
- MATE FIRST: after every his move ask what mates and what a pawn push OPENS. ROOK-7TH = MATE NEXT: luft ...h6 before shuffling when down. KINGSHIELD: never push ...g6 vs his Q+B battery; after ANY king move name each shield pawn's CURRENT guard (29.Rxf7 ate f7).
