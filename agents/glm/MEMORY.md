## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (fatal 7.d3??, T36R4): a pawn push that removes a guard (g3 unguarded f3) leaves the hole until something REPLACES it - Qd1 was blocked by my own e2, so Nf3 hung from move 3 until 6...e4 exf3 collected it, while 7.d3 'resolved' nothing (d3 attacks e4, not f3; defending my attacked PAWN is not defending the attacked PIECE). Every move ask: which of my pieces have NO current guard?
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL (fatal 33.Bd6??): 'Bd6 defends d4 via e5' is two bent diagonals, not a line. From its own square a bishop sees only its two straight diagonals (from d6: a3-b4-c5 and e5-f4-g5-h6 - never d4). Name the full unbroken line to the target; a bend = no guard.
- ROOK LINES: (a) never park a piece alone on an enemy rook's open rank/file to 'block' it - the rook attacks its FIRST blocker (38.Bd4?? Rxd4); (b) never walk my rook onto an enemy rook's file (24.Rb1??); (c) with two enemy rooks name EACH one's file/rank/diagonal before moving (21.Rxa7?? forgot Ra8 standing on that very file).
- KNIGHTS GO ON THE SWEEP LIST (fatal 17.Bxe4??, T36R3): when any of my pieces lands on a square, the attacker list is PAWNS + SLIDERS + EVERY ENEMY KNIGHT (c5-knight: a4/a6/b3/b7/d3/d7/e4/e6). A sweep without knight jumps is not a sweep.
- SHUFFLE = GUARD DUTY LOSS (T36R3 31.Rc1?): before ANY move, shuffles included, name what the moved piece WAS guarding on the old board. Rc1 stepped off the d-file and boxed Bd2 lost its only guard -> Rxd2.
- QUEEN DESTINATION SWEEP - PAWNS AND SLIDERS BOTH (fatal 5x): before ANY queen move - quiet, capture or CHECK - list (1) every enemy PAWN attacking the destination (each covers 2 squares) AND (2) every enemy SLIDER's line traced square by square. The a2-g8 diagonal has eaten my queen twice (his Qe3: e3-d4-c5-b6 all EMPTY). A check answered by a pawn capture = Q for P.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY: recite the 8 squares before trusting any knight guard. c6: a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 - NEVER d5. f6: d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7 - never e5.
- CHECKS PASS THE SAME SWEEP (44.Rc8+?? Bxc8): before delivering ANY check run the full destination sweep (pawns, sliders, knights, king-flights). Be6's empty e6-d7-c8 diagonal ate the rook.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION: the piece being MOVED never defends its own destination (16...Be5?? 'defended by Bf6' = the mover itself). Exclude the mover from guard counts.
- BLOCKED DEFENDER: before any capture or move onto a square 'defended' by my bishop/rook, walk the line SQUARE BY SQUARE and name each occupant. A guard through my own piece DOES NOT EXIST (13...exd5? T35R3; 7.Nxe4?? T35R4).
- KINGSHIELD PUSHES vs Q+B BATTERY: g7 guards h6 AND f6. Never push ...g6 (or move off f7) while his queen + dark bishop aim at my castled king - ...g6 bared h6 -> Qxh6+ Qxg6+ mate (T36R1). Shuffle solid or trade his bishop first.
- FINISH THE NAMED SEQUENCE: 'capture, then recapture' means the recapture is the move played (13.Rd1?? T35R1 lost a piece). After HIS capture the first question is 'what recaptures'. GOOD models: Rxd8/Rxe7 (T35R3), Kxf1! (T36R4).
- DESTINATION ATTACKERS: list every enemy piece attacking the destination (pawns one push away included) and all my guards from real geometry, excluding the mover. 20...Bd7?? had zero guards vs Rc7; model 24...Re8 guarded the attacked Be7.
- FORK REALITY: my check/fork is real only if nothing captures my piece on its destination - scan every slider's line first (8.Nc7+?? Qxc7). Verify HIS checks too: 37.Qh5+?? was undefended, Kxh5 won his queen.
- RECAPTURE FIRST / ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: after every trade re-count guards of attacked pieces; a piece whose only guard is my queen is lost when his second attacker lands (13.g5?? Bxd4, T34R3).
- MATE FIRST: after every his move ask what mates and what a pawn push OPENS (T36R1 23...g6; T34R1 23.exf6 -> Qxg7#; 26...Qe1# back rank).
- DOWN MATERIAL: trade pieces, no grabs, blockade, shuffle - vs SF expect NO repetition rescue. The discipline works AT the blunder moment (T36R4 held N+P down for 20+ moves) but cannot rescue a hole; one untraced move ends it. In R-vs-B endings the bishop stays OFF the rook's open lines; the KING blockades passers.
- MATERIAL FROM THE BOARD: recount after every capture (T35R4 m15 'one pawn down' = full knight down). Legality too: Nxe5 with NO knights = instant rejection; know my remaining pieces.

- LEGALITY: destination empty and not mine, path empty, SAN fits; doubled rooks need disambiguation (Rad8/Red8); castling needs both squares empty and home pieces moved; pawns attack DIAGONALLY FORWARD only. BOXED PIECE RULE: list a walled-in piece's legal squares once, never retry it - after ONE rejection redraw and pick a DIFFERENT piece/idea.
