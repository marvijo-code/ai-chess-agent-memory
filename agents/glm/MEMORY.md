## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- PAWN MAP FIRST ON ANY 'TRADE' (T44R4 10...Ned5??): a trade offer is judged on EVERY capturer of the contact square, PAWNS BEFORE PIECES - a pawn capture is piece-for-pawn, and no later recapture restores the piece. - PAWN-DIAGONAL KILL LIST: before ANY of my pieces lands beside an enemy pawn - capture, retreat, quiet queen move, king step - write the pawn's square FROM THE BOARD, then its BOTH forward capture diagonals, and check my destination. Rank-5/6 pawns near my arriving piece are tripwires; up material changes nothing. Then walk EVERY enemy slider's line square by square. HIS KING'S 8 ADJACENT SQUARES are capture squares too (44.Bd7?? Kxd7).
- DEFENDED IS A COUNT, NOT A CLAIM: for every square a plan leans on, write HIS attackers (queen lines square by square + ALL EIGHT knight jumps of every nearby knight) vs NAMED defenders; the king counts once. T42R5: f2 attackers Qh4+Nd3 v king = 2:1, so Qxf2+ froze Kxf2. EVERY NEW ENEMY KNIGHT = spell its 8 jumps the move it lands (d3: b1/b2/c1/c5/e1/e5/f2/f4).
- SELF-BAN (T44R2 12...Nxe4??): a move my own scan called losing is DEAD; a re-evaluation RE-LISTS ALL HIS ATTACKERS from the board, sliders' diagonals FIRST - defenders-only is not a count.
- CHECK SIEGE: after every check ask (a) what captures the checker (KING included), (b) what my king move ABANDONS - name what the old square guarded (18.Kh1 -> Re1 unguarded -> Qxe1+), (c) what lies BEHIND the checker - a discovered rook behind a check ends the game (21...Be5+ had Rg8 behind, 22.f4??
