## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- PLANNED RECAPTURE MUST BE LEGAL - PIN CHECK (T38R3 21...Kf8??): when my answer to his check/capture is '...pawn takes', FIRST trace the slider line BEHIND the pawn (toward my king or any piece of mine). His Qg3 on the g-file pinned my g7 to Kg8: gxf6 did not exist, Kf8 walked into 22.Nxe8 = a whole rook. Same family as T37R7's exd5 fantasy. If the planned recapture is pinned, recalculate the ENTIRE reply before moving - do not play the check's 'logical' continuation.
- HIS LAST MOVE'S FULL RAYS BEFORE MY PLAN (T38R2 24...Qa6): walk EVERY line from his new square against EVERY piece of mine - his Qa6 hit my a5-pawn AND my Qd3; I answered only the pawn hit. First question: 'what does his move attack - including MY queen?' before 'what do I attack?'. WORKED in T38R3: 16...Bxc6! and 18...Qd7! (his Bf3's f3-c6 diagonal, open once e4 cleared).
- ATTACKED PIECE -> COUNT ATTACKERS ON THE DESTINATION (T38R2 15.Nxe5??): guarded is not safe - count HIS attackers v MY defenders on the new square (2 v 1 died).
- PAWN GEOMETRY IN CAPTURE CHAINS: before ANY plan ending in '...then my pawn takes', name the capturing pawn's TWO FORWARD diagonals on the CURRENT board, BOTH colours: d6 hits c5/e5; e5 hits d4/f4 (black) AND d6/f6 (white - 14...d6?? freed nothing, 15.exf6); f7 hits e6/g6; c5 hits b4/d4; b5 hits a4/c4. If the destination is not one of the two, the plan does not exist. The rule MUST run while lost and merely reacting.
- DESTINATION SWEEP ON EVERY MOVE, quiet and developing included: (a) every adjacent enemy PAWN's two capture squares, (b) every enemy KNIGHT's 8 squares, (c) every enemy slider's line walked square by square, (d) king flights. Recite FROM THE BOARD - a sweep recalled from memory is not a sweep.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY: c6 hits a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 (never d5); f6 hits d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; d7 hits b6/b8/c5/e5/f6/f8. 'Defender left' != 'undefended': after ANY exchange recount ALL CURRENT attackers.
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN (15...Qe7??): after HIS capture name each of MY pieces hitting the destination, line traced; play the even recapture rather than a counterattack or retreat.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS: after ANY enemy capture sweep the capturer's lines FROM ITS NEW SQUARE.
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE (14...Bc5??, 19...Bg5??): count HIS defenders first; the attack must win on recapture arithmetic. Four recurrences.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES (13...Bd7??): never park a piece on an enemy pawn's forward diagonals.
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL (33.Bd6??): name the full unbroken line; a bend = no guard.
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): a push that removes a guard leaves the hole until replaced. Every move: which of my pieces/pawns have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE (20.Rd8??): before any rook move to an open file walk the defending line THROUGH MY OWN PAWNS; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION + BLOCKED DEFENDER: the mover never defends its own destination; a guard line with any piece on it DOES NOT EXIST.
- SHUFFLE = GUARD DUTY LOSS: name what the moved piece WAS guarding on the old board.
- QUEEN SALLY NEEDS MY RECAPTURER (22.Qxc6 = Q+B for Q+P): before ANY queen move name the piece that recaptures for me.
- FORK REALITY: my check/fork is real only if nothing captures my piece on its destination (8.Nc7+?? Qxc7).
- ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: a piece whose only guard is my queen is lost when his second attacker lands (13.g5?? Bxd4).
- MATE FIRST: after every his move ask what mates and what a pawn push OPENS. ROOK-7TH = MATE NEXT: luft ...h6 before shuffling when down. KING ON THE RIM, BOXED BY OWN PAWNS, HIS Q+R = Qg8# next (T38R3): make luft EARLY and stay off the edge; never ...g6 vs his Q+B battery.
- FINISH THE NAMED SEQUENCE: the named recapture must BE the move played.
- WHEN DOWN, HUNT HIS UNDEFENDED ROOKS (32.Bxb7!, 44.Kxf5!): a rook defended only by a pawn one square beside it or off its bishop's true diagonal is a free piece.
