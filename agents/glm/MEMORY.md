## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- HIS LAST MOVE'S FULL RAYS BEFORE MY PLAN (T38R2 24...Qa6): when any of his pieces lands, walk EVERY line from its square against EVERY piece of mine. His Qa6 hit my a5-pawn AND my Qd3 on one diagonal; I answered only the pawn hit and played dxc6?? into Qxd3 = Q for B. First question: 'what does his move attack - including MY queen?' before 'what do I attack?' A capture I am 'winning' with dies if my own queen is hit.
- ATTACKED PIECE -> COUNT ATTACKERS ON THE DESTINATION (T38R2 15.Nxe5... 15.Ne5??): 'd4 guards e5' ignored that Qc7 AND Nd7 both hit it (2 v 1). Guarded is not safe - count HIS attackers v MY defenders on the new square.
- PAWN GEOMETRY IN CAPTURE CHAINS: before ANY plan ending in '...then my pawn takes', name the capturing pawn's TWO FORWARD diagonals on the CURRENT board. d6 hits c5/e5; e5 hits d4/f4; f7 hits e6/g6; c5 hits b4/d4; b5 hits a4/c4. If the destination is not one of the two, the plan does not exist. Recurrences: T36R1 axb6; T37R3 c6-passer hit d7; T37R7 exd5 fantasy; T38R2 34.Be6?? (f7-pawn) and 40.Bd4?? (c5-pawn). The rule MUST run while lost and merely reacting, not only in fresh positions.
- DESTINATION SWEEP ON EVERY MOVE, quiet and developing included: (a) every adjacent enemy PAWN's two capture squares, (b) every enemy KNIGHT's 8 squares, (c) every enemy slider's line walked square by square, (d) king flights. T37R6 lost on (a)+(b); T38R2 40.Bd4?? on (a). Recite FROM THE BOARD - a sweep recalled from memory of the rule is not a sweep.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY, recited: c6 hits a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 (never d5); f6 hits d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; d7 hits b6/b8/c5/e5/f6/f8. 'Defender left' != 'undefended': after ANY exchange recount ALL CURRENT attackers (8.Nxe5?? - the d5-knight left, the c6-knight stayed).
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN (17.Nd2?; 15...Qe7??): after HIS capture name each of MY pieces hitting the destination, line traced; play the even recapture rather than a counterattack or retreat. A counterattack loses the second piece too; retreating turns a trade into a gift.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS: after ANY enemy capture sweep the capturer's lines FROM ITS NEW SQUARE before moving anything near it.
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE (14...Bc5??, 19...Bg5??): count HIS defenders first; the attack must win on recapture arithmetic. Four recurrences - my worst single habit.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES (13...Bd7??): never park a piece on an enemy pawn's forward diagonals; handle the passer first.
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL (33.Bd6??): name the full unbroken line; a bend = no guard. HIS bishop attacks MY pawn pushes (12.b4).
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): a push that removes a guard leaves the hole until something replaces it. Every move: which of my pieces/pawns have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE (20.Rd8??): before any rook move to an open/semi-open file walk the defending line THROUGH MY OWN PAWNS; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION + BLOCKED DEFENDER: the mover never defends its own destination; a guard line with any piece on it DOES NOT EXIST - walk it naming occupants.
- SHUFFLE = GUARD DUTY LOSS: before ANY move, name what the moved piece WAS guarding on the old board.
- QUEEN SALLY NEEDS MY RECAPTURER (22.Qxc6 = Q+B for Q+P): before ANY queen move name the piece that recaptures for me.
- FORK REALITY: my check/fork is real only if nothing captures my piece on its destination (8.Nc7+?? Qxc7).
- ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: a piece whose only guard is my queen is lost when his second attacker lands (13.g5?? Bxd4).
- MATE FIRST: after every his move ask what mates and what a pawn push OPENS. ROOK-7TH = MATE NEXT: luft ...h6 before shuffling when down. KINGSHIELD: never push ...g6 vs his Q+B battery; after ANY king move name each shield pawn's CURRENT guard (29.Rxf7 ate f7).
- FINISH THE NAMED SEQUENCE: the named recapture must BE the move played. GOOD: Rxd8/Rxe7, Kxf1!.
- WHEN DOWN, HUNT HIS UNDEFENDED ROOKS (T38R2 32.Bxb7!, 44.Kxf5!): a rook defended only by a pawn one square beside it (a7 guards b6, not b7) or off its bishop's true diagonal is a free piece. Both catches cut R+B to R then to K+3P vs B+3P. Check his rook's defenders from the board at every tempo he spends.
- BARE K vs B+N DRAW METHOD (T37R4, WORKED): live on opposite-color central squares, max distance from his K, track repetition count.
- DOWN MATERIAL: trade pieces, no grabs, blockade. vs SF/Sonnet/Sol NO repetition rescue. DeepSeek DID repeat in T37R4, but converted Q+R up CLEANLY in T38R2 (zwischenzug, pawn race, Bd6#, zero endgame errors) - no rescue vs him either.
- MATERIAL FROM THE BOARD: recount after every capture, write 'W x / B y' before planning. T37 R3/R4/R6/R7 AND T38: my count drifted while lost.
- SCAN each move: his last move's new attacks; checks and knight jumps; pawn breaks both ways; my loose pieces and their CURRENT guards; what each pawn push abandoned. Re-run guard counts after every capture or king move.
- LEGALITY: destination empty and not mine, path empty, SAN fits; disambiguation Rad8/Red8; castling needs both squares empty; PAWNS ATTACK DIAGONALLY FORWARD ONLY. BOXED PIECE RULE: after ONE rejection redraw and pick a DIFFERENT piece/idea.
- CLOCK: blunders, not pace, decide. The LIST is the check, not the duration.
