## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- OCCUPANT OF THE CAPTURE SQUARE, NAMED FROM THE BOARD NOW (T38R5 14...Qxb6?? = Q for N; T38R4 13.Qxd7?? = Q for P): before EVERY capture state what piece of HIS stands on the destination AFTER HIS LAST MOVE - a square he just moved to holds the NEW arrival, not the old piece. Name colour AND type, plus all its guards.
- HIS NEW KNIGHT = ALL 8 JUMPS, ASK WHICH TWO TARGETS: a landed knight often hits TWO loose units at once (14.Nb6! hit Bd7 AND Ra8). 'Verified one thing' is not a sweep.
- ATTACK IS NOT A RETREAT ORDER (T39R1 13...b5??): before a move that attacks his piece, list THAT piece's captures on the proposed board. A knight jumps OVER my attacking pawn: ...b5 hit Na4 while b5 covered only a4/c4 - Nxc5 took my queen. If his piece has a capture worth my target, answer HIS attack first (13...Qb6/Qc7/...d6). The SAME ...b5 won a pawn in T38R5 because his Na4 had no queen capture - verify on THIS board, never from memory.
- HIS CHECK = HUNT CAPTURES OF THE CHECKER (T39R1 21...Kxe7!): after any check, before retreating, ask what takes the checking piece. His Qe7+ was unguarded - Kxe7 turned Q+R down into only R down.
- MATERIAL = FULL PIECE LIST FROM THE BOARD, NOT DELTA ARITHMETIC (four wrong claims in T38R5 alone): after every capture write both armies (W: Q RR BB NN Px / B: ...). Recount BEFORE choosing any plan.
- PLANNED RECAPTURE MUST BE LEGAL - PIN AND REACH (T38R3 21...Kf8??; T39R1 27...Rc6?? 'Kxc6' from g6): trace the slider line behind the pawn/square, THEN confirm the recapturer is ADJACENT or on an open line to the square. If it fails, recalculate the ENTIRE reply before moving.
- HIS LAST MOVE'S FULL RAYS BEFORE MY PLAN (T38R2 24...Qa6): walk EVERY line from his new square against EVERY piece of mine; first question: 'what does his move attack - including MY queen?'.
- ATTACKED PIECE -> COUNT ATTACKERS ON THE DESTINATION (T38R2 15.Nxe5??): guarded is not safe - his attackers v my defenders.
- PAWN GEOMETRY IN CAPTURE CHAINS: d6 hits c5/e5; e5 hits d4/f4 (and d6/f6 for white); f7 hits e6/g6; c5 hits b4/d4; b5 hits a4/c4; g7 hits h6/f6; h6 hits g5. Name the capturing pawn's TWO forward diagonals on the CURRENT board.
- DESTINATION SWEEP ON EVERY MOVE, quiet and developing included: (a) adjacent enemy pawns' two capture squares, (b) enemy knights' 8 squares, (c) enemy sliders' lines walked square by square, (d) king flights. Recite FROM THE BOARD - a sweep recalled from memory is not a sweep.
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY: c6 hits a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 (never d5/b6); f6 hits d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7; d7 hits b6/b8/c5/e5/f6/f8. After ANY exchange recount ALL CURRENT attackers.
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN - and it IS a trade only with a named recapturer on BOTH sides (15...Qe7??; T38R5 'Qxb6 trade'). Finish the named sequence; the named recapture must BE the move played.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS; ATTACKING MOVES ABANDON GUARDS (T38R4 16.Re1?? left f2 bare -> Qxf2#).
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE: count HIS defenders first; must win on recapture arithmetic. Four recurrences.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES: never park a piece on an enemy pawn's forward diagonals (13...Bd7??).
- GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS UNBLOCKED LINE; SELF-GUARD ILLUSION: the mover never defends its own destination.
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): every move, which of my units have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE through my own pawns; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines.
- QUEEN SALLY NEEDS MY RECAPTURER; ONLY-QUEEN GUARD: lost when his second attacker lands.
- MATE FIRST: after every his move ask what mates and what a pawn push or capture OPENS. PAWN SHELL CAPTURES ARE MATE OPENERS (28...gxh6 -> Qg7#). Q IN MY CAMP + un-lufted boxed king = mate next; make luft EARLY, never ...g6 vs his Q+B battery. His caging method (T39R1): Re1 seals the e-file + his own pawns cage my king -> Rb3#.

- BARE K vs B+N DRAW METHOD (T37R4): opposite-colour central squares, max distance, track repetition count.
- DOWN MATERIAL: trade pieces, no grabs, blockade. No repetition rescue vs SF/Sonnet/Sol/DeepSeek.
- SCAN each move: his last move's new attacks; checks and knight jumps; pawn breaks both ways; my loose pieces and CURRENT guards; what each pawn push abandoned; PIN check; WHO occupies each capture square.
