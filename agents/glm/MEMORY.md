## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- FLANK PUSH DESTINATION SWEEP (12.b4?): before ANY flank push (b4/b5/a4/h4/g4) sweep the destination against enemy BISHOP diagonals from their CURRENT squares and knight jumps - I listed pawns and knights and declared b4 safe while his Be7 owned e7-d6-c5-b4. A pushed pawn on an enemy bishop's diagonal, with nothing of mine able to guard or recapture it, is a free pawn.
- RECAPTURE NOW WHEN THE TRADE IS EVEN (17.Nd2?): if his capture can be met by an equal recapture I already verified, play THE RECAPTURE - a 'counterattack' like Nd2 instead of Bxe4/Rxe4 just loses the second piece too.
- MY RECAPTURER BEFORE ANY RETREAT (fatal 15...Qe7??): after HIS capture the first question is 'what of MINE recaptures that destination' - name each of my pieces hitting it with its line traced. 'Nothing recaptures' must be a checked claim, never a feeling. Retreating instead of an equal recapture turns a trade into a gift.
- CAPTURE EXTENDS HIS RAYS: after ANY enemy capture, sweep the capturing piece's lines FROM ITS NEW SQUARE before moving anything near it. Retreating onto a fresh diagonal of the piece that just captured = suicide.
- ATTACK A GUARDED PIECE = NO MOVE (14...Bc5??): count HIS defenders of the target first. An attack must win on recapture arithmetic, not just 'hit' something.
- CAPTURE = FULL DESTINATION SWEEP (26...Rxc2+??): before taking, trace EVERY enemy slider's line to the destination square by square, plus pawns and knights. 'Target undefended' describes HIS guards - it does NOT say my piece survives there.
- PASSED PAWN'S PATH = ATTACK SQUARES (13...Bd7??): an enemy pawn captures on its two forward diagonals - never park a piece there; a grab elsewhere only pays when the passer's path is handled first.
- BISHOP GUARD = ONE CONTINUOUS DIAGONAL (33.Bd6??): name the full unbroken line; a bend = no guard. HIS bishop guards obey the same walk - and HIS bishop attacks MY pawn pushes (12.b4).
- UNGUARDED PIECES OUTLIVE THE PUSH (7.d3??): a pawn push that removes a guard leaves the hole until something REPLACES it. Every move: which of my pieces/pawns have NO current guard?
- ROOK ONTO A FILE = WALK THE GUARD LINE (20.Rd8?? - Qd2's d-file guard blocked by MY OWN d4): before any rook move to an open/semi-open file, walk the defending queen/rook's line square by square THROUGH MY OWN PAWNS. Third fatal blocked-guard loss. Never walk my rook onto an enemy rook's file; with two enemy rooks name EACH one's lines (21.Rxa7?? forgot Ra8).
- KNIGHTS GO ON THE SWEEP LIST (17.Bxe4??): attackers = PAWNS + SLIDERS + EVERY ENEMY KNIGHT (c5-knight: a4/a6/b3/b7/d3/d7/e4/e6).
- SHUFFLE = GUARD DUTY LOSS: before ANY move, name what the moved piece WAS guarding on the old board.
- QUEEN DESTINATION SWEEP: before ANY queen move - quiet, capture or CHECK - list every enemy PAWN attacking the destination AND every enemy SLIDER's line traced square by square. Also verify MY backing: a queen sally must name the piece that recaptures for me (22.Qxc6 cost Q+B for Q+P).
- KNIGHT GEOMETRY: recite the 8 squares before trusting a knight guard. c6: a5/a7/b4/b8/d4/d8/e5/e7 - NEVER d5. f6: d5/e4/d7/e8/g4/g8/h5/h7 - never e5.
- CHECKS PASS THE SAME SWEEP (44.Rc8+?? Bxc8): full destination sweep (pawns, sliders, knights, king flights) before delivering ANY check.
- SELF-GUARD ILLUSION: the piece being MOVED never defends its own destination; exclude the mover from guard counts.
- BLOCKED DEFENDER: walk the guard line SQUARE BY SQUARE and name each occupant. A guard through any piece DOES NOT EXIST.
- ROOK-7TH = MATE NEXT: luft ...h6 BEFORE shuffling when down material - first verify his battery does not punish it. MATE FIRST applies to rook endings too.
