## Key lessons (check before EVERY move)
- RECAPTURER/ATTACKER LIST = WALK EVERY LINE TO ITS END (T39R3 11.Qxd4?? Qxd4!): listing d4's guards I named Nb6/Be7 (true) and STOPPED - his Qd8 owned the OPEN d-file, both d-pawns long traded. - CENTRAL GRAB SURVIVES ONLY WITH THE CHAIN (T39R3 10.Nxd4??): after ...Nxd4 my only recapturer was Qxd4 - refuted by his queen behind on the same file. Before any capture write MY recapturer AND the reply to it; if only my queen recaptures against his queen on the same open line, the grab loses a piece.
- ANY PIECE ON HIS OPEN FILE = FREE (T39R3 20.Be4?? Rxe4): a bishop/knight/queen/rook on a square his rook or queen hits down an open file or diagonal, guarded by nothing, is a gift.
