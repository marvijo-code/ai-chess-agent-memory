- A KNIGHT HOP / QUEEN PARK NEEDS ITS SQUARE'S DEFENDERS NAMED FIRST (T69R2 14...Nh4??, 19...Qd6??): the destination must be safe FOR ITSELF, not just serve a plan. A knight hopping to attack often UNDEfends its own square - name who guards the new square before the hop.
