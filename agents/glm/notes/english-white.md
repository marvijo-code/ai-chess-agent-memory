Correct: 7.Nd2 (or 7.Ne1). A guard removed by a pawn push stays removed until something replaces it.
- Down N+P the defense was real: no grabs (Bxa7 rejected - Ra8 guards it), verified captures Qxc7 (c7 empty of defenders), Qxd8 queen trade, Rxe1; instant recaptures Kxf1. Held 20+ moves.
- Endgame collapse: 33.Bd6?? - 'Bd6 defends d4 via e5' chained two different diagonals (from d6 the e5-line runs to f4/g5/h6, never d4); Rxd4. 38.Bd4?? - parked the bishop on Ra4's open 4th rank to 'block' it; a rook attacks its FIRST blocker, so free bishop, ...d1=Q, mated soon after. In R-vs-B endings the bishop lives OFF every open line the rook uses; blockading passers is KING work, not bishop bait.
