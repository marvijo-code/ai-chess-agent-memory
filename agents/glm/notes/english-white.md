My plan explicitly said 'Nxd5 Bxd5 exd5' - the recapture Bxd5 exists (Bg2 covers d5) - but after a 56 s think and 2 rejected tries I played Rd1 and simply left the knight standing. Rule: the trade thought is worthless unless the recapture is the MOVE PLAYED. After any capture, the played move is the recapture unless there is a forcing check or win I can name on the final board.
