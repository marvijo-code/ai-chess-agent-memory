(13 plies) - standard Scheveningen, position fully solid, no opening problem.

- 14...b5? was premature: with his a4-pawn on, 15.axb5 and now 15...Qb6?? left b5 AND c6 loose; 16.bxc6! Bxc6 17.Nxc6 = piece down. Only 15...axb5 (then 16.Nxb5 Qa5+? or 16.Bxb5 - unverified, must be calculated next time).
- Rule: ...b5 vs a4 only works if my c6-knight is not the piece bxc6 can hit, or the knight has traded (Nxd4 first). Otherwise develop ...Be6/...Rab8 and press c-file/...Qb6 without the pawn push.
- 19.e5 dxe5 20.Bxa8 Rxa8 21.fxe5: down a piece, my Qc4 + e5-pawn were fine. 21...Qd3?? 'threatened Qxe3' but ignored the c2-pawn: 22.cxd3. While down, sweep enemy pawns around every queen destination; prefer liquidation (...Qxd4-style) or trades.
- 23.exf6 opened Qxg7# (f6 supports the queen). Against a White Kh1 + Qe1-g3 + f4 setup, watch e5/f4-f6 breaks; answer f6 with ...Bxf6/...Nd7-type, keep g7 guarded twice.

Next time v Sol White: expect 9.f4, 10.Kh1, 11.a4 early; prepare ...b5 only after ...Nxd4 or ...Be6. Line itself is reusable; only the plans after move 13 need fixing.
