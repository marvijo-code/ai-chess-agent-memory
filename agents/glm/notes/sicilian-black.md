- 16.e5 dxe5 17.fxe5 attacked my Nf6. 17...Qxe5?? 'won a pawn' because I believed Nf6 guarded e5. A KNIGHT ON F6 NEVER ATTACKS E5 (its real squares: e4, d5, d7, e8, g4, g8, h5, h7). - Sound alternatives to verify next time: 17...Nd5 (18.Bxd5 cxd5, a clean pawn down but equalish, knight out of e5-attack), or avoid 16...dxe5 and consider ...Ne8/...Nd5 ideas when his e5 lands. 16...dxe5?! (SF) invited the f4/fxe5 hit on Nf6.
- 18...Bd6?? 'kicked' his queen but my Bd7 blocked Rd8's guard of d6 -> 19.Qxd6. Never count a sliding guard without tracing the file/diagonal through MY OWN pieces. When down, prefer trade/consolidation over an activity gesture.
- 14...bxc6?! (SF): 14...Bxc6 keeps the bishop pair choice; bxc6 is playable (opens b-file) but check c6's guards after any e5 break.
- General: I hallucinated a whole positive narrative while down ('3 pawns down drawish rook ending') - recount material FROM THE BOARD after every capture before choosing a plan.
